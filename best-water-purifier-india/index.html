Best Water Purifier in India (2026): How to Choose by Your Water

The single most important fact about buying a water purifier in India: the right one depends on your water, not on which brand advertises hardest. Spend ₹100 on a TDS meter first — it tells you whether you need RO at all, and can save you thousands on a machine you don't.

Start here: test your tap water's TDS (total dissolved solids, in ppm) with a cheap handheld meter. That one number decides whether you need RO, UV, UF, or a combination — everything below flows from it.

RO vs UV vs UF — what each actually does

TypeRemovesNeeds power?Best when
RO (Reverse Osmosis)Dissolved salts — lowers TDS, heavy metalsYesHigh-TDS / hard / borewell / tanker water
UV (Ultraviolet)Bacteria & viruses (doesn't change TDS)YesMicrobially unsafe but low-TDS water
UF (Ultrafiltration)Suspended particles, cysts, bacteriaNoLow-TDS municipal water, or no-electricity use

Most branded units combine these — RO+UV+UF for the toughest water. The mistake to avoid is buying RO when you don't need it: on already-soft water, RO strips useful minerals and wastes litres per glass.

What to check before you buy

  • Your TDS — the deciding factor. Over 500 → RO; under 200 → UV/UF. Test it.
  • Storage tank size — 7–8 L suits a family of 4–6; smaller is fine for 2–3 or where supply is continuous.
  • Mineraliser / TDS controller — on RO units, this puts healthy minerals back and improves taste.
  • Water-wastage ratio — standard RO wastes ~3 L per 1 L purified; water-recovery models waste much less.
  • Service network — in smaller cities, a brand with local technicians beats a slightly better spec you can't get serviced.
  • Running cost — filters ~₹3,000–5,500/yr for RO, plus any AMC. Factor this into the real cost, not just the sticker.

Frequently asked questions

Do I really need an RO purifier?

Only if your TDS is high. Test it first. Over ~500 ppm (common with borewell/tanker water) you need RO; under ~200 ppm (treated municipal supply) RO is unnecessary — UV or UV+UF is enough, and RO just wastes water and strips minerals.

What's the difference between RO, UV and UF?

RO lowers TDS (dissolved salts); UV kills bacteria and viruses without changing TDS; UF removes particles and cysts with no electricity. Hard, unsafe water often needs RO+UV+UF together; clean low-TDS water needs only UV or UF.

What TDS level is safe to drink?

BIS treats up to 500 mg/L as acceptable, and 50–150 ppm usually tastes best. Below 50 is over-purified; above 500 is where RO earns its place. A ₹100–200 TDS meter tells you yours.

What does it cost to run?

Filters cost roughly ₹3,000–5,500 a year for an RO unit, plus any annual maintenance contract. RO also wastes water unless the model has recovery technology — factor both into the true cost.
