Costs range from ₹40 lakh (smaller cities) to ₹2.5 crore (premium pan-India brands).

Hearing aids — a 30-minute test can change life quality

Modern hearing aids are nearly invisible (in-the-canal designs), Bluetooth-enabled, and start at ₹15,000/pair for entry digital. Mobility aids & wellness products

Falls are the leading cause of injury among Indian seniors. Simple aids prevent 70% of falls — grab bars in bathrooms, anti-slip mats, walking sticks with shock absorbers, and rollator walkers. Wheelchair brands like Karma and Ottobock dominate the premium market; cheaper Indian brands like Vissco and Smart Care work well for short-term needs.

Frequently asked questions

Are retirement communities the same as old-age homes?

No. Old-age homes (vrudhasrams) are typically charity-run, dormitory-style, low-cost facilities for seniors with no family support. Retirement communities are premium independent-living apartments where seniors own or rent their own home — more like resort-style senior-only housing.

What's the average cost of in-home elderly care in India?

For an 8-hour daily attendant: ₹15,000-25,000/month. For 24/7 attendant care (with rotating staff): ₹35,000-60,000/month. Skilled nursing (medication, wound care): add ₹500-1,500/visit on top.

Do health insurance policies cover elderly care?

Standard health insurance covers hospitalization. Long-term elder-care or in-home daily attendants are NOT covered by most retail policies. A few specialized senior-only plans (Star Senior Citizens Red Carpet, Niva Bupa Senior First) include some homecare benefits — read the fine print.

Can hearing aids be returned?

Most major brands offer 30-90 day trial periods in India. Always insist on a trial — fit and noise environment vary widely. Don't buy hearing aids without an audiogram (free at most ENT clinics).

What about emergency response systems?

Apple Watch (Series 4+) has fall detection that auto-calls emergency services. For non-Apple users, Emoha and KITES offer 24/7 emergency response subscription (₹500-1,500/month) with a wearable button.
