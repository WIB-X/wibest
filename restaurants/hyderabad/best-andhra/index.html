How to spot great Andhra in Hyderabad

  • Always order with extra ghee on the side: Andhra meals are designed for ghee mixed into rice.
  • Pace your spice. Start with sambar and rasam before tackling the gunpowder and pickle.
  • Banana leaf service is a sign of authenticity. Stainless steel plates in the South are a tourist tell.
  • Andhra meals are unlimited by tradition. Ask for refills before the staff clears your plate.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best Andhra restaurant in Hyderabad?

Top-rated Andhra restaurants in Hyderabad serve unlimited meals on banana leaf with 8-12 items including 3+ varieties of chillies, gunpowder, ghee, and authentic rasam. Our top picks above are ranked on these traditional service signals.

What is the Andhra meals price in Hyderabad?

A standard Andhra unlimited meal in Hyderabad costs Rs 200-400. Premium Andhra fine-dining (Rayalaseema-style) can go up to Rs 600-800 per person. Most great Andhra spots offer the best value at lunch with their signature meals.

Is Andhra food too spicy?

Andhra food is famously spicy but most restaurants in Hyderabad adjust the heat for the local palate. Always specify "less spicy" if you prefer milder. The Rayalaseema and Nellore styles are spicier than the coastal Andhra style.

When is the best time to eat Andhra meals?

Andhra unlimited meals are a lunch tradition. Most restaurants in Hyderabad serve their signature meals between 12pm and 3pm. Dinner menus often skip the unlimited meals option in favour of a la carte.
