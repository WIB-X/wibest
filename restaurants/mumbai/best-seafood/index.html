How to spot great Seafood in Mumbai

  • Ask what came in today. The best seafood spots change their menu based on the morning catch.
  • Whole fish (tava-fried or tandoori) reveals freshness better than gravied fillets.
  • Avoid fresh seafood claims at inland restaurants. Coastal cities have a real freshness edge.
  • Pair with traditional accompaniments: appam with coconut stew, neer dosa with fish curry, sol kadhi after.

Frequently asked questions

Which restaurant has the freshest seafood in Mumbai?

The freshest seafood in Mumbai comes from restaurants close to the coast or fishing harbour. Restaurants that change their menu daily based on the morning catch are usually the freshest. Our top picks above include several catch-of-the-day spots.

What is the seafood price in Mumbai?

Seafood in Mumbai ranges from Rs 300 for fish thalis at neighbourhood spots to Rs 2,000+ for premium prawns, crab, or lobster at fine-dining restaurants. Fish curry meals are usually the best value.

Which seafood style is local to Mumbai?

Mumbai's coastal cuisine has its own style — Konkan, Malvani, Goan, Mangalorean, or Kerala depending on location. The restaurants ranked above represent the regional seafood traditions native to Mumbai.

Is the seafood in Mumbai good for tourists?

Most top seafood restaurants in Mumbai cater well to tourists with English menus, mild-spice options, and wine lists. Coastal fishing-village spots offer the most authentic experience but may have limited language flexibility.
