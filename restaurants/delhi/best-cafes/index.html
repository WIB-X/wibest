Frequently asked questions

Which is the best cafe in Delhi for working?

Cafes that allow long sittings without pressure to leave usually have power outlets at most tables, decent Wi-Fi, and a workspace-friendly noise level. Our top picks above include several work-friendly cafes — look for ones tagged with "Continental" or "Cafe" cuisine.

What time do cafes in Delhi open?

Most cafes in Delhi open between 8am and 10am for breakfast service. Specialty coffee cafes typically open at 8am, while brunch-focused spots may open at 10-11am. Most stay open until 11pm.

Which cafes in Delhi serve the best coffee?

Specialty coffee cafes in Delhi typically source single-origin beans from Chikmagalur, Coorg, or Araku Valley. Look for cafes that name their roaster, list bean origins, and offer pour-over or V60 alongside espresso. The cafes ranked above are evaluated on coffee quality, not just ambience.

Are these cafes in Delhi pet-friendly?

Many cafes in Delhi are now pet-friendly, especially those with outdoor seating. Always call ahead to confirm — policies vary and some only allow pets in specific seating areas. Look for cafes near residential pockets which tend to be more pet-welcoming.
