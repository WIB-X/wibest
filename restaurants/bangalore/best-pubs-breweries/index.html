Frequently asked questions

Which microbrewery in Bangalore has the best craft beer?

Top-rated microbreweries in Bangalore typically brew 4-8 styles in-house, offer flights for sampling, and rotate seasonal beers. The breweries listed above are ranked by beer quality, consistency, and atmosphere — not just popularity.

What time do pubs and breweries close in Bangalore?

Pubs and microbreweries in Bangalore typically close at 11:30pm or 12am as per local liquor licensing rules. Last orders are usually called 30 minutes before closing. Weekend closing times can vary based on the venue.

Which brewery in Bangalore is best for groups?

Several breweries listed above accommodate groups of 6-10 with reservations. Larger venues like Byg Brewski work better for groups of 10+. Always book in advance for Friday and Saturday evenings.

Do these pubs in Bangalore serve food?

Yes — most modern microbreweries in Bangalore have full kitchens serving pub grub: pizza, burgers, kebabs, and tapas-style sharing plates. The food quality varies; the spots ranked highest above are known for both beer AND food.
