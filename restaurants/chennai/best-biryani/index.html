How to spot great Biryani in Chennai

  • Order it freshly made: biryani is best within 30 minutes of dum cooking. Reheated biryani loses its layered flavour.
  • Look for separate grain. The best biryani has each rice grain distinct, never mushy or clumpy.
  • Ask about marination time. 4-6 hours minimum is what separates great biryani from average.
  • Watch for hidden additives. Authentic biryani uses no orange/red food colour: natural saffron and turmeric do the work.
