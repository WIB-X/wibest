Frequently asked questions

Which is the best South Indian restaurant in Chennai?

The top South Indian restaurants in Chennai are ranked above based on consistency, authenticity, and reviews. Most great South Indian places are family-run institutions decades old — the kind of consistency that earns top ratings is hard to fake.

Where can I get the best dosa in Chennai?

The best dosas in Chennai come from spots that focus on tiffin specifically — masala dosa, ghee roast, and rava dosa. Look for places that grind their batter daily and serve dosas hot off the tava, not pre-cooked.

Is South Indian food in Chennai vegetarian only?

Most traditional South Indian restaurants in Chennai are pure vegetarian. Some Chettinad and coastal Karnataka restaurants serve non-vegetarian options including chicken Chettinad, Mangalorean fish curry, and biryani. We tag this clearly above.

What is a typical South Indian thali price in Chennai?

A standard South Indian unlimited thali in Chennai ranges from Rs 150 at neighbourhood spots to Rs 400-600 at premium restaurants. Saturday-Sunday lunch usually has special thali pricing across most restaurants.
