Frequently asked questions

Which is the best Bengali restaurant in Kolkata?

The top Bengali restaurants in Kolkata are ranked above. Authentic Bengali cuisine uses mustard oil, panch phoron, and sequenced courses (shukto first, then dal, fish, meat, ending with sweets) — restaurants that follow this format usually deliver the best experience.

What is a typical Bengali meal price in Kolkata?

A Bengali set meal in Kolkata ranges from Rs 350-800 at mid-range restaurants. Premium Bengali fine-dining can be Rs 1,500+. Sunday lunches with Bengali mutton dishes are usually a special pricing slot.

What should I order at a Bengali restaurant?

For first-timers: shorshe ilish (hilsa in mustard) if available, or chingri malai curry (prawn coconut curry). Add aloo posto or shukto for vegetarian sides. Finish with mishti doi and sandesh.

Where to find best fish in Bengali restaurants in Kolkata?

The freshest fish at Bengali restaurants in Kolkata is usually rohu, katla, bhetki, or pabda. Hilsa (ilish) availability is seasonal and highly priced. Ask the server which fish was sourced fresh that day.
