Where can I get the best dosa in Coimbatore?

The best dosas in Coimbatore come from spots that focus on tiffin specifically — masala dosa, ghee roast, and rava dosa. Look for places that grind their batter daily and serve dosas hot off the tava, not pre-cooked.

Is South Indian food in Coimbatore vegetarian only?

Most traditional South Indian restaurants in Coimbatore are pure vegetarian. Some Chettinad and coastal Karnataka restaurants serve non-vegetarian options including chicken Chettinad, Mangalorean fish curry, and biryani. We tag this clearly above.

What is a typical South Indian thali price in Coimbatore?

A standard South Indian unlimited thali in Coimbatore ranges from Rs 150 at neighbourhood spots to Rs 400-600 at premium restaurants. Saturday-Sunday lunch usually has special thali pricing across most restaurants.
