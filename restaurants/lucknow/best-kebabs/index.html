The top kebab spots in Lucknow are ranked above. Authentic kebab making is craft work — the meat grind, marination, skewer technique, and tandoor temperature all matter. Generations-old kebab houses usually beat newer establishments.

What is the price range of kebabs in Lucknow?

Kebab platters in Lucknow range from Rs 250 at neighbourhood spots to Rs 800-1,500 at fine-dining restaurants. Galouti kebab and tunday kebab specialists charge premium for the technique.

What is the difference between galouti, kakori, and seekh kebab?

Galouti is a melt-in-mouth shaped patty (Lucknow speciality), kakori is a fine-paste seekh kebab (named after Kakori town), and standard seekh kebab is coarser-textured. Each requires different techniques and meat preparation.
