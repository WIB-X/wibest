About WIB

What WIB is, who runs it, and why it exists.

What is WIB?

WIB (wibest.in) is India's independent comparison platform for hospitals, restaurants, schools, colleges, electronics, cars, and travel. Built and maintained by a single Indian founder using AI tools, we rank options by data and editorial judgement, never by paid placement. Launched in 2025.

As of May 2026: 463 hospitals across 36 cities, 605 restaurants across 28 cities, 10 research reports, 140+ open datasets.

Who runs WIB?

One Indian founder, AI-augmented editorial. No staff. No VC funding. No advertiser influence on rankings. The founder personally writes every research report, verifies every hospital/restaurant record, and responds to every reader email at hello@wibest.in.

Full team structure + decision rights + accountability at /team/.

Why is WIB different from other Indian comparison sites?

Three things: (1) No paid placements, ever. Most Indian "Top 10" lists are paid SEO. WIB rankings are editorial. (2) Open data. We publish every dataset as CSV + JSON under CC-BY 4.0 so anyone can verify our numbers. (3) Solo + AI-augmented. No team forced to chase advertiser money to make payroll.

Editorial principles documented at /methodology/.

Is WIB profitable? How does it make money?

Currently: ad revenue (Google AdSense) + affiliate links on relevant product comparisons (Amazon Associates, Flipkart via EarnKaro, Cuelinks). Disclosed at /affiliate-disclosure/. Affiliate links don't influence rankings — they're added after editorial selection, only on products that won on merit.

No, WIB is not yet profitable. Reach (organic search + press) is the current bottleneck, not features.

Data & methodology

How we verify what we publish.

How does WIB verify hospital data?

Every hospital is added via a 7-step editorial protocol:

  • Source identification (verifiable public website OR registry listing)
  • Name + address cross-check against the hospital's own website
  • NABH accreditation check against official registry
  • Bed count sourced from hospital website or annual report
  • Specialty list capped at top 5 the hospital highlights itself
  • Rating normalisation from public review aggregates (Google, Practo)
  • Quarterly re-verification

Full protocol with limitations callouts at /methodology/.

How are restaurant ratings calculated?

Aggregated from publicly available review platforms (Google, Zomato) on a 5-point scale. We don't accept unverifiable user-submitted reviews. Restaurants below 4.0 rating are generally excluded — WIB's coverage is "top-rated," not exhaustive. Cuisine tagging is editorial (max 4 cuisines, not the "Multi-Cuisine + every cuisine" pile that diluted Zomato's data).

How often is WIB's data updated?

Quarterly review cycle for every record. Combined endpoints (/data/csv/wib-hospitals-all.csv, /data/json/hospitals.json) always reflect the latest verified snapshot. Major dataset versions are logged at /data/changelog/.

Closed businesses are removed; new businesses are added; ratings refresh; NABH status changes propagate.

What does WIB explicitly refuse to do?

Six explicit refusals:

  • No paid placements
  • No fake reviews — only aggregated public-source data
  • No scraping of competitors (Zomato/Practo are sanity checks, not source)
  • No anonymous editorial — all reports bylined
  • No hidden affiliate manipulation
  • No clickbait headlines unsupported by data

Full list at /methodology/.

Using our data

For journalists, researchers, developers, and analysts.

Can I use WIB data in my research / journalism / app?

Yes — for free. All open datasets are published under CC-BY 4.0 at /data/. You can use them commercially, modify them, redistribute them. The only requirement is attribution.

Suggested attribution: Data source: WIB Open Data (wibest.in/data/), CC-BY 4.0.

Is there a public API?

Yes. JSON endpoints with documented schema, code samples in JavaScript / Python / curl, recipe library, and CORS enabled. No signup, no API key, no rate limits. Full docs at /api/.

How should I cite WIB in academic research?

BibTeX format:

@misc{wib_hospitals_2026, author = {WIB Editorial}, title = {WIB India Hospitals Dataset 2026}, year = {2026}, url = {https://wibest.in/data/}, license = {CC-BY-4.0}}

Email hello@wibest.in with your published paper or report and we'll list you on /press-coverage/.

Can I embed WIB's data on my own website?

Two ways:

  • WIB Score badge at /embed/score/ — if your hospital or restaurant is featured on WIB, you can embed our verified rating badge.

Indian comparison topics

Common questions across hospitals, dining, education that come up in our research.

What does NABH accreditation mean?

NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) is India's flagship hospital quality-accreditation body. NABH-accredited hospitals are independently verified on 600+ patient-safety, infection-control, surgical-care, and credentialed-staff measures.

In WIB's dataset: 97% of metro hospitals hold NABH vs 68% in tier-3 cities — a 29-point quality gap documented in our Beyond the Metros report. Full term at /glossary/#nabh.

Are biryani and pulao the same?

No. Both are layered rice dishes but: biryani uses the dum-cooking method where raw or partially-cooked meat and rice are layered in a sealed pot and slow-cooked together. Pulao is cooked in one step, like a pilaf. Biryani is heavier, more aromatic, more festive; pulao is lighter and faster.

Full term at /glossary/#biryani-pulao.

CBSE vs ICSE vs IB — which is best?

It depends on the child's path:

  • CBSE: largest network, uniform across India, closely tracks JEE/NEET. Best for families that may relocate or aim for engineering/medical entrances.
  • ICSE: more rigorous on English and humanities. Best for arts/language careers.
  • IB: globally portable, strong critical thinking. Expensive (Rs 5-15L/year). Best for families planning international university applications.
  • State boards: regional-language instruction at lower fees, aligned with state-level entrances.
What's the best Indian city for remote work in 2026?

Overall: Bangalore. Cost-to-quality: Indore. The honest ranking with methodology is in our Best Indian Cities for Remote Workers 2026 report. Top 5: Bangalore, Indore, Pune, Hyderabad, Mysore. Mumbai and Delhi are intentionally not in the top 10 because their cost-of-living drags the composite for remote workers.

To get a personalised match, take the 6-question quiz.

Should I move from a metro to a tier-3 Indian city?

Move to tier-3 if: your healthcare needs are routine, you're remote-work eligible, kids are under 13 and don't need IB schools yet, spouse's career doesn't require metro density. The cost saving compounds into Rs 1+ crore corpus differential over 10 years.

Stay in metros if: you need oncology/transplant care, your kids need IB/Cambridge schools or are within 5 years of IIM/ISB applications, your career requires daily in-person network density.

Contributing

How readers, businesses, journalists, and developers help WIB improve.

How do I report an error on WIB?

Email hello@wibest.in with the page URL and the specific error. Include a source for the corrected information (the business's own website, official registry, etc.). We respond within 5 working days and either correct, remove, or explain.

Major corrections are documented in the changelog. We don't silently edit published research.

My business is featured on WIB. How do I update the listing?

Email hello@wibest.in from a verifiable business email (matching domain or LinkedIn-listed business email). Include the WIB page URL + the change requested + supporting source (your website / official registry / annual report).

If you've also installed the WIB Score badge on your site, mention it — we'll feature you on /press-coverage/.

How do I add a new hospital / restaurant / business?

Email hello@wibest.in with: business name, full address, website, type (multi-specialty hospital / restaurant / etc.), and for hospitals — NABH accreditation status. We review editorially and add based on our methodology criteria. Multi-specialty + verifiable + currently operational businesses are added; single-specialty clinics and dental chains are excluded.

I'm a journalist. How do I get story access / quotes?

Press kit at /press/ — logos, fact sheet, founder bio, ready-to-use quotes, recent reports.

For interviews or custom data, email hello@wibest.in with "Press Inquiry" in subject + your publication + deadline. Response within 24 hours.

Can I write a guest report or contribute editorially?

Not currently — editorial is intentionally single-byline to maintain coherence. But you can: (1) suggest research questions, (2) point out gaps in our coverage, (3) share data we should know about, (4) join the weekly newsletter to follow what's shipping. Email hello@wibest.in with what you'd want to see.
