Pune vs Hyderabad for Living - 2026 Comparison

Pune vs Hyderabad is the steady-vs-rising tech city debate. Pune offers a mature IT-services + auto manufacturing hub with strong educational pedigree. Hyderabad offers booming MNC product hiring, better infrastructure, and India's biryani capital status. Here is the head-to-head.

The verdict

Choose Pune if you value calmer pace, slightly cheaper living, weekend nature access (Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar), and proximity to Mumbai for trips. Choose Hyderabad for senior MNC tech roles (highest 2026 hiring momentum), better infrastructure (working metro, closer airport), and biryani capital status. Hyderabad has the salary edge (~15-20% higher senior packages). Pune wins lifestyle for families; Hyderabad wins career for tech professionals 5+ years experience.

Frequently asked questions

Which has higher tech salaries - Pune or Hyderabad?

Hyderabad pays roughly 15-20% more for senior tech roles (5+ yrs) due to Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Apple presence. Pune is competitive for early-career and IT services roles. The gap widens with seniority.

Which is cheaper - Pune or Hyderabad?

Pune is marginally cheaper (5-7%) on rent and overall living. Both are in the affordable tier-1 bracket. The cost difference is small enough that lifestyle preference should drive the choice.

Which has better weather - Pune or Hyderabad?

Pune wins on summer (12-35°C vs Hyderabad 17-40°C). Both similar in winter and monsoon intensity. Pune is generally drier with more pleasant evenings. Weather alone tips many family decisions to Pune.

Hyderabad metro vs Pune metro?

Hyderabad metro is fully operational with 70 km of lines covering HITEC City, financial district, and core areas. Pune metro is partial — limited routes operational, expansion underway. Hyderabad wins decisively today.

Where is food scene better?

Hyderabad has stronger restaurant scene led by biryani and Mughlai (see Hyderabad restaurants). Pune has Maharashtrian heritage food and growing modern dining (see Pune restaurants). Hyderabad has more depth; Pune has more variety relative to size.
