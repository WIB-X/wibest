Beyond the Metros: Tier-3 Hospital Access

40 multi-specialty hospitals tracked across Bhopal, Nagpur, Mysore, Vadodara, and Patna. Headline: NABH accreditation runs at 68% in tier-3 vs 97% in metros — a 29-point quality gap. Cancer care and transplants still pull patients to metros; the AIIMS expansion is changing that.

50 verified restaurants across 5 tier-3 cities. Headline: regional-anchored restaurants outscore generic multi-cuisine 4.4 vs 4.3. Vinayaka Mylari's butter dosa in Mysore (4.6) is the highest-rated single dish in our entire 605-restaurant dataset.
