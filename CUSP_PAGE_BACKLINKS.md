## Track A — Resource-page & roundup inclusion (finance + education pages)

The single most realistic white-hat link for a "best X" page: get added to a page that already lists resources on that topic, or to an existing "best X" roundup that omitted you.

### Prospecting — paste these into Google (do a batch, save the good URLs)

**Mutual-fund-app page:**
```
"best mutual fund app" india 2026 -wibest.in
"best investment apps" india intitle:2026 blog
inurl:resources "mutual fund" india personal finance
"best sip app" OR "best mutual fund app" india "list" -site:play.google.com
```
**NEET / IIT-JEE pages:**
```
"best NEET coaching" india blog 2026 -wibest.in
"best IIT JEE coaching" india "list" OR "top" 2026
inurl:resources ("NEET" OR "JEE") preparation students
"study resources" NEET JEE site:.edu.in OR site:.ac.in
```

### What you're looking for
- Blog roundups published in the **last 12 months** that list "best mutual fund apps / NEET coaching" but **don't include you** → pitch inclusion (Template A1).
- Resource / useful-links pages on finance blogs, school/college sites, counselor sites → pitch addition (Template A2).
- Roundups with a **broken/dead link** in the list → offer your page as the replacement (Template A3). This converts best.

## Track B — City / relocation links (the 10 city-living pages)

These are the **easiest links to earn** because cost-of-living / salary-comparison data is genuinely citable, and relocation content always needs it.

### Prospecting
```
"moving to bangalore" guide cost of living
"relocating to hyderabad" OR "moving to hyderabad" blog
"bangalore vs hyderabad" cost of living -wibest.in
site:reddit.com/r/bangalore cost of living moving   (referral + discovery, links are nofollow)
"HR relocation guide" india bangalore hyderabad
```

### Angles that get a yes
- **Relocation / expat / "moving to <city>" guides** — offer your side-by-side cost + salary comparison as a citable stat (Template B1).
- **Company HR / "life at <city>" pages** — they link neutral third-party cost data readily.
- **City subreddits & forums** — nofollow, but drive real referral traffic and often get scraped into do-follow content later.

## Track C — The compounding play: one linkable data asset (highest ROI)

Commercial pages are hard to earn links to directly. The fix used successfully for the hospitals/restaurants reports: **publish a data asset that journalists/bloggers cite, and internally link it to the commercial pages.** The link authority flows internally to NEET/IIT-JEE/MF-app.

**Build one of these next (each ~1 day, then pitch via the existing journalist list):**
1. **"India Coaching Fee Index 2026"** — table of NEET + JEE coaching fees by institute & city (you already have the data on the two coaching pages). Angle: *"What it really costs to crack NEET/JEE in 2026 — and the tier-3 vs Kota gap."* Internally links to both coaching pages. Fits your existing `EducationAffordabilityIndex` report style.
2. **"Salary vs Cost of Living: which metro leaves you richer, 2026"** — powered by your 10 city-living pages. Angle: *"A ₹15L salary in Bangalore = ₹X in take-home lifestyle vs Hyderabad."* Internally links every city page. This is the one that also feeds the "bengaluru hyderabad salary comparison" query directly.

These are the same link-bait mechanic that already earned coverage — just pointed at the cusp pages.

## Ready-to-send templates

> Send from your **personal** address, not hello@. Keep under 150 words. Personalise the first line — reference their actual article. One follow-up after 5 days, then stop.

```
Either way, thanks for a genuinely useful piece.

[Your name]
wibest.in — independent India comparison platform
```
