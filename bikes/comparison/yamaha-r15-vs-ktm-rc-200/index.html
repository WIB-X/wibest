The verdict

Buy the Yamaha R15 V4 if you want a learner-friendly sports bike with refined power delivery, best-in-class handling, and Yamaha's legendary reliability. Buy the KTM RC 200 if you can handle aggressive ergonomics and want maximum performance for the money. R15 is the bike most riders should choose; RC 200 is for committed sportbike enthusiasts.

Frequently asked questions

Which is faster - R15 V4 or RC 200?

KTM RC 200 wins outright on top speed (160 vs 140 kmph) and 0-100 (10.5 vs 13.5 sec) due to bigger engine. R15 holds its own on twisty roads where its lighter weight and better-balanced chassis matter more.

Is R15 V4 good for daily commute?

R15 V4 is the most comfortable sports bike for daily commute thanks to clip-ons that are higher than RC 200, narrower seat, and better fuel efficiency. Still aggressive for traffic but manageable. RC 200 commute is genuinely uncomfortable beyond 30 minutes.

Service cost - Yamaha vs KTM?

Yamaha service costs are 20-30% lower than KTM and parts more available. KTM service is premium-priced especially for engine work. R15 has lower 5-year ownership cost by approximately Rs 30,000-40,000.

Should I buy R15 V4 or wait for R15 V5?

R15 V4 is a mature, well-sorted product. V5 if/when launched will have similar engine but updated electronics. If you need a bike now, V4 is the safe choice. If you can wait 12-18 months and want latest tech, hold for V5.
