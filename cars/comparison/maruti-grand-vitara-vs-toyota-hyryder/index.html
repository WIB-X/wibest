Maruti Grand Vitara vs Toyota Hyryder

Grand Vitara and Hyryder are the rebadged twins from Maruti-Toyota partnership. Same platform, same engines, different badging. The interesting comparison is service experience, resale, and feature packaging at the same trim.

The verdict

Both cars are mechanically identical. Choose Grand Vitara for Maruti broader service network, lower service cost, and slightly better resale. Choose Hyryder if you have a Toyota dealer nearby, want extendable Toyota warranty (up to 8 years), or prefer Toyota service experience. The Hyryder is marginally cheaper at top variant. Net: Grand Vitara wins on rational ownership cost; Hyryder wins on warranty and dealer experience for Toyota loyalists.

Frequently asked questions

Are Grand Vitara and Hyryder the same car?

Mechanically yes — same engine, same platform, same hybrid system. Cosmetic differences only (front grille, badging, alloy design). Performance and reliability are identical.

Which has better mileage - mild or strong hybrid?

Strong hybrid delivers 27.97 km/l ARAI vs mild hybrid 21.11 km/l. Real-world: strong hybrid 22-25 km/l, mild hybrid 17-19 km/l. Strong hybrid premium is Rs 1.8-2 lakh — pays back in fuel savings within 4-5 years for typical commuters.

Why is the Global NCAP rating only 3-star?

Both cars score 3-star adult occupant protection (5-star eligible structure but lower scores in side-impact + child protection tests). Newer compact SUVs (Nexon, XUV 3XO) achieve 5-star — Grand Vitara/Hyryder safety is the weakest dimension.

Maruti Nexa or Toyota dealer for service?

Maruti Nexa wins on service network breadth and lower per-visit cost. Toyota dealers are fewer but offer extendable warranty up to 8 years (worth Rs 25,000-40,000 over Maruti package). Pick based on dealer proximity.
