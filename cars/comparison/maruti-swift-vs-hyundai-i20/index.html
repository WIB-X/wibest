Maruti Swift vs Hyundai i20

Maruti Swift and Hyundai i20 are India's top two premium hatchbacks. Swift owns the volume game with proven reliability and lowest service costs; i20 wins on features, build quality, and feel-good factor. Here's a head-to-head on what matters.

The verdict

Buy the Swift if you want the lowest 5-year ownership cost, fuel efficiency, and resale value — it is the safer, more sensible choice. Buy the i20 if you want a more premium feel, better tech, and a stronger driving experience — especially the 1.0L Turbo variant which delivers genuine punch. Family use leans Swift; enthusiast or city-only use leans i20.

Frequently asked questions

Which is more reliable - Swift or i20?

Swift has the edge on long-term reliability based on India's real-world data and Maruti's service network. i20 is reliable too but its more complex features mean slightly more potential failure points.

Which has better mileage in real-world use?

Real-world mileage: Swift petrol manual delivers 18-22 km/l, i20 petrol manual delivers 14-17 km/l. Swift wins by 3-5 km/l in everyday Indian conditions.

Is the i20 1.0L Turbo worth the extra cost?

For enthusiastic drivers - yes. The Turbo i20 delivers genuine performance, paddle shifters with the DCT, and feels like a hot hatch. For pure commuters, the standard 1.2L is enough.

Which has better safety - Swift or i20?

Latest Hyundai i20 (2024+) carries a 3-star Global NCAP rating. Maruti Swift's rating is older (1-star) but the new-gen Swift expected in 2026 may score better. For safety-conscious buyers today, i20 wins.
