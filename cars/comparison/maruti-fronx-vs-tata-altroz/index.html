Maruti Fronx vs Tata Altroz

Fronx and Altroz are different in spirit but compete for the same buyer: premium hatchback with strong design. Fronx is a crossover-coupe with raised stance; Altroz is the safest hatchback in India. Both target Rs 8-12 lakh buyers.

The verdict

Buy the Maruti Fronx if you want a stylish crossover-coupe shape with raised stance, Maruti reliability, and the best mileage in this segment. Buy the Tata Altroz if safety rating is non-negotiable (only 5-star Global NCAP hatchback in India) and you want diesel or EV options. Fronx is the heart pick (looks, fuel economy); Altroz is the sensible pick (safety, engine variety).

Frequently asked questions

Is Fronx a hatchback or SUV?

Fronx is technically a crossover-coupe — built on Baleno hatchback platform but with raised stance, plastic cladding, and sportier design. Functionally it is a tall hatchback. Not a true SUV.

Why is Tata Altroz a 5-star safety champion?

Altroz scored 5-star Global NCAP for adult occupant protection — the only premium hatchback in India with this rating. Tata uses thicker steel structure, 6 airbags standard, and engineering safety as a core brand value.

Which has better mileage - Fronx CNG or Altroz CNG?

Fronx CNG: 28.5 km/kg ARAI. Altroz CNG: 26.2 km/kg ARAI. Fronx wins on CNG mileage. Both have factory-fitted CNG with twin tanks for boot space optimization.

Should I get diesel Altroz or wait for Altroz EV?

Diesel is being phased out for cost reasons (BS6.2 norms add Rs 1-1.5 lakh to diesel price). Altroz EV makes more sense for new buyers expecting 5+ year ownership. Diesel Altroz only worth it for high-km drivers who do not want CNG.
