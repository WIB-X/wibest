Key Factors in Picking an SUV

In 2025, "SUV" covers everything from a Tata Punch to a Toyota Fortuner. Under ₹15 lakh on-road, you are in the compact and mid-size SUV space — the most competitive segment in India.

1. Hyundai Creta — ₹11-₹15 lakh on-road

Still the India darling. The 2024 facelift added Level 2 ADAS, a 10.25" dual-screen, and a refined 1.5 turbo-petrol DCT. Rides well, looks bold, and has the best resale value in the segment.

2. Skoda Kylaq — ₹7.89-₹14 lakh

The surprise of 2025. Built on Skoda's MQB-A0 IN platform (same as Kushaq). 1.0 TSI, 25 ADAS features, and a 5-star Bharat NCAP rating at ₹8 lakh ex-showroom. Unbeatable value.

3. Kia Seltos — ₹11-₹15 lakh on-road

Sharp, well-built, and the only one here with a proper panoramic sunroof under ₹14 lakh. 1.5 turbo makes 160 bhp — the most power in the segment.

4. Maruti Grand Vitara Hybrid — ₹13-₹15 lakh on-road

27.97 kmpl claimed, 23-25 kmpl real-world. Toyota's strong hybrid system, Maruti's service network, and decent boot space. Best monthly fuel bill in the segment.

5. Mahindra Thar 3-door — ₹11-₹14 lakh on-road

Not practical. But if you want a weekend off-roader that also commutes, nothing else here does real 4x4 under ₹15 lakh. The new petrol AT is surprisingly liveable in city.

Safety

Bharat NCAP ratings: Kylaq 5 stars (adult), Thar 4 stars, Creta 3 stars, Seltos yet to be retested, Grand Vitara 5 stars in Global NCAP.

Final Verdict

The Hyundai Creta is still the all-rounder winner. For value and safety, buy the Skoda Kylaq. For highway mileage, Grand Vitara Hybrid. For off-road fun, Thar 3-door.

Frequently asked questions

Which SUV under ₹15 lakh has the best mileage?

Maruti Grand Vitara Hybrid — 22-25 kmpl real-world. Second is the Creta diesel at 17-19 kmpl.

Is the Skoda Kylaq reliable in India?

Built on the localised MQB-A0 IN platform with 95% Indian parts, it should match Volkswagen Taigun's reliability — which has been solid post-2022.

Which SUV is safest under ₹15 lakh?

Skoda Kylaq and Maruti Grand Vitara both have 5-star crash ratings. Thar is 4-star.

Should I buy petrol or diesel in 2025?

Diesel still makes sense if you drive 1,500+ km/month. Otherwise petrol or hybrid is cheaper over 5 years.
