Best earbuds under ₹2,000 in India (2025)

Three years ago, ₹2,000 earbuds were a compromise. Today, you get active noise cancellation, 30-hour total battery, hi-res audio support, and IPX5 water resistance at this price. The competition between OnePlus, boAt, Noise, and JBL has pushed quality up significantly. Here are the best options tested by our team.

Top picks

1. OnePlus Nord Buds 3 Pro — Best overall

Price: ₹1,999

The Nord Buds 3 Pro is the best all-round earbud under ₹2,000. The 49 dB ANC is genuinely effective at blocking AC hum and office chatter. Sound signature is balanced with punchy bass that doesn't drown out mids. Call quality is clear thanks to three-mic ENC. 10 mm dynamic drivers with LDAC support means music sounds detailed even from lossless Spotify tracks. 44-hour total battery life (10 hr buds + 34 hr case).

2. boAt Airdopes 141 ANC — Best ANC for price

Price: ₹1,299

At ₹1,299, the boAt Airdopes 141 ANC is extraordinary value. The ANC isn't as deep as the Nord Buds 3 Pro but for commuters and WFH workers, it's effective enough for its price. Bass-forward tuning suits Indian music — Bollywood, Punjabi beats, and EDM sound punchy. 48-hour total battery.

3. Noise Buds VS104 Max — Best for calls

Price: ₹1,499

If you're primarily on calls all day, the Noise Buds VS104 Max with quad-mic setup and Environmental Noise Cancellation delivers the clearest call quality under ₹2,000. Colleagues won't know you're on earbuds in a moderately noisy room. Sound quality is secondary but adequate.

4. JBL Wave Flex — Best brand reliability

Price: ₹1,999

JBL's service network and brand reliability make the Wave Flex worth considering. Open-ear design with JBL Deep Bass mode is comfortable for all-day wear. JBL headphones app support for EQ customisation is a rare feature at this price.

Things to check before buying

Codec support: AAC is fine for iOS users. Android users benefit from aptX or LDAC for better audio quality. SBC is the fallback — decent but not great for audiophiles.

ANC depth: Budget ANC works on steady background noise (AC, train, aircraft cabin). It cannot cancel sudden sharp sounds or conversations. Set realistic expectations.

Fit matters most: Sound quality is useless if the earbuds don't seal properly in your ears. If possible, test in-store before buying, or buy from Amazon/Flipkart with easy returns.

Frequently asked questions

Do earbuds under ₹2,000 have good ANC?

Yes — the OnePlus Nord Buds 3 Pro at ₹1,999 offers 49 dB ANC that genuinely reduces background noise. boAt Airdopes 141 ANC at ₹1,299 is also effective for its price. Budget ANC works well on steady noise like AC hum and traffic but not sudden sounds.

Which is the best earbud under ₹2,000 for bass lovers?

boAt Airdopes 141 ANC has the most bass-heavy tuning under ₹2,000, followed by JBL Wave Flex. For balanced sound with decent bass, the OnePlus Nord Buds 3 Pro is better.

Are cheap earbuds worth it or should I spend more?

For casual use, earbuds under ₹2,000 are more than good enough. If you're an audiophile, listen for 2+ hours daily, or need deep ANC for flights, consider spending ₹4,000–₹8,000 for Sony WF-C700N or CMF Buds Pro 2.
