1. iQOO Neo 10R

Snapdragon 8s Gen 3, 7K mm² vapor chamber, 144Hz AMOLED with 300Hz touch sampling. We ran BGMI at 90 FPS Ultra HDR for 40 minutes with a drop of only 4 FPS. The phone gets warm but not alarming. Game Space features include 4D vibrations, Monster mode, and bullet-time capture.

2. POCO X7 Pro

LiquidCool 4.0 chamber is genuinely the best in this price band. Sustained Genshin at 60 FPS for an hour in a 32°C room — no phone under ₹30k comes close. Downside: slightly bland design.

3. Realme GT 6T

6,000-nit peak display is overkill but useful under Indian summer sun. Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3 handles everything at 90 FPS but throttles slightly earlier than the iQOO.

4. OnePlus Nord 4

5,500 mAh battery means 6-7 hours of BGMI on a single charge. OxygenOS 15 is the cleanest skin in this price band. Slightly weaker gaming chip than iQOO but better everyday phone.

5. Motorola Edge 50 Fusion — Dark Horse

At ₹23,999 with the Snapdragon 7s Gen 2, this is the budget pick. Clean near-stock Android, Pantone-verified display, and surprisingly decent cooling.

Settings to Unlock Max FPS in BGMI

For 90 FPS in BGMI on any phone above, enable Developer Options, set GPU renderer to Skia, and enable "Force 90Hz". In BGMI graphics settings, choose Smooth + Extreme+ for best ping-to-FPS ratio in competitive.

Final Verdict

The iQOO Neo 10R is the best gaming phone under ₹30,000 in 2025. The POCO X7 Pro is the alternative if you game for 2+ hours at a stretch and need sustained thermals. The Motorola Edge 50 Fusion is the budget pick at ₹23,999.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run BGMI at 90 FPS under ₹30,000?

Yes. iQOO Neo 10R, POCO X7 Pro, and Realme GT 6T all support 90 FPS Ultra HDR with stable frametimes.

Which is better for gaming — Snapdragon or Dimensity?

Both are comparable in 2025. Snapdragon 8s Gen 3 has a slight edge in Unreal Engine games. Dimensity 9200+ is stronger in multi-core.

Does a cooling fan really help?

Yes — a ₹700 clip-on fan can keep a phone 5-8°C cooler and prevent throttling in 30+ minute sessions.

Is a 144Hz display necessary for gaming?

Not for BGMI (capped at 90). But it helps for Call of Duty Mobile at 120 and general scrolling smoothness.
