India's Mattress-in-a-Box Revolution

D2C brands like Wakefit, SleepyCat, and Sunday disrupted the ₹15,000 crore mattress industry. They cut middlemen, ship vacuum-packed, offer 100-night trials. Sleepwell and Duroflex (legacy) responded with online stores. Customer wins.

Wakefit Dual Comfort — Best Value

Flippable mattress: one side soft, other side hard. ₹11,999 for queen 6-inch. 1 crore+ Indian families have bought Wakefit. 100-night trial, 10-year warranty.

SleepyCat Original — Best D2C Premium

3-layer construction (cooling + memory + support foam). Cooler than Wakefit due to gel-infused top layer. Excellent for hot Indian summers.

Sleepwell Spring Cool Tech — Best Spring Mattress

Brand trust since 1971. Pocketed springs reduce partner disturbance. Cool tech fabric resists heat. 10-year warranty. Available widely in physical stores too.

Duroflex Strength Plus — Best for Back Pain

Orthopedic-grade firmness. Recommended by orthopedic surgeons. 6-zone support. Heavier (~25 kg for queen) so installation takes effort.

How to Choose

  • Memory foam: Best pressure relief, contours to body. Can sleep hot — choose gel-infused.
  • Spring: Bouncy, cooler, traditional feel. Pocket springs > Bonnell springs.
  • Latex: Most durable (15-20 years), naturally cool. Expensive.
  • Hybrid: Combines layers. Often the best of multiple worlds.
  • Firmness: Side sleepers need medium, back/stomach sleepers need firm.

Trial Period & Warranty

Most online brands offer 100-night trials with full refund. Sleepwell and Duroflex offer 30-night trials in their direct stores. Warranties: 7-10 years standard. Be aware many warranties cover defects only, not natural sagging.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best mattress brand in India?

Wakefit for value, Sleepwell for spring, Duroflex for orthopedic, SleepyCat for premium memory foam, Sunday for natural latex.

Is memory foam good for hot weather?

Standard memory foam sleeps hot. Choose gel-infused or copper-infused memory foam — Wakefit Dual Comfort and SleepyCat have cooling layers.

Memory foam vs spring mattress — which is better?

Memory foam for pressure relief and motion isolation. Spring for bounce and breathability. Hybrid mattresses combine both.

Does Wakefit really last 10 years?

Most users report 5-8 years before noticeable sag. The 10-year warranty covers manufacturing defects only, not natural softening.
