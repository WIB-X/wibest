Section 80D — The Second-Biggest Tax Saver

After 80C's ₹1.5 lakh, Section 80D offers the largest deduction to salaried taxpayers. Combined limit up to ₹1 lakh if you cover yourself + family + senior citizen parents.

Self + spouse + children (age <60)₹25,000
Self + spouse + children (if you're 60+)₹50,000
Parents (age <60)₹25,000

Preventive health check-ups (within above limits)₹5,000

Maximum 80D benefit for typical family: ₹25,000 (self+family) + ₹50,000 (senior parents) = ₹75,000 deduction. At 30% tax slab: ₹23,400 saved annually.

Best-case scenario (both you and parents 60+): ₹50,000 + ₹50,000 = ₹1 lakh deduction. At 30% slab: ₹31,200 saved.

Preventive Health Check Benefit

Section 80D includes ₹5,000 deduction (within overall limit) for preventive health checks. Most insurers include annual free check-up; if yours doesn't, standalone checks at Apollo/Thyrocare/Lal Path cost ₹2,500-4,500. Keep receipts.

Pro Tips

  • Pay premium by cheque/card/online — cash payment disqualifies 80D deduction (except preventive check-up under ₹5,000).
  • Keep premium receipts for 8 years — tax department can ask for proof.
  • Spouse's employer-provided health insurance also qualifies for 80D — but only if you paid part of the premium.
  • Corporate group health insurance paid by employer doesn't count for 80D — only your own paid premiums.

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim 80D for my spouse's health insurance?

Yes — if you paid the premium. If your spouse paid, they claim it in their own ITR. Joint payment: whoever paid a majority claims.

Is parent health insurance premium deductible if they don't live with me?

Yes. Residence doesn't matter. You must be the premium-payer, and parents must be defined as per Income Tax Act (biological or legally adopted).

Can I claim 80D for my mother-in-law or father-in-law?

No. Only your own parents. For in-laws' health insurance, you need a separate tax-paying family member (e.g. your spouse) to claim.

What if I pay my parent's premium in cash?

Cash payment disqualifies the deduction, except for preventive health check-ups up to ₹5,000. Always pay via cheque, online transfer, or card.

Is top-up health insurance also eligible for 80D?

Yes. Top-up and super top-up plans are health insurance and qualify for 80D, within the overall family/parents limit. Great cost-efficient way to enhance cover: ₹5L base + ₹25L top-up together cost less than ₹30L base plan.
