The Short Answer

Buy a split AC if: you own your home, plan to stay 5+ years, have a wall that faces outside, and value quieter operation. 85% of new installations in India are splits.

Buy a window AC if: you rent, your window already has a cutout, your budget is tight, or you're installing in a room with no outdoor wall access. Lower upfront cost wins for many.

When Window AC Wins

  • Renters: Taking a split AC with you requires re-gassing refrigerant (₹4,000-6,000), re-installation (₹2,500-4,500), and new copper piping if the new home has different run-length. Window ACs uninstall in 30 minutes and slot into any standard window cutout.
  • Homes with no outdoor wall access: Interior rooms (e.g. middle bedroom in row houses, inner apartments).
  • Low budget: A good window AC is ₹6,000-10,000 cheaper than an equivalent split, including installation.
  • Small rooms (≤150 sq ft): Window ACs are efficient at this size and noise is less noticeable.

When Split AC Wins

  • Bedrooms and master rooms: 35 dB vs 55 dB is the difference between sleeping and not sleeping. Splits are whisper-quiet.
  • Modern apartments: Builders rarely design windows to fit ACs anymore. Splits are the default.
  • Larger rooms (150-250 sq ft): Better airflow distribution via louvred indoor unit.
  • Premium cooling tech: Inverter, AI cooling, air purification, Wi-Fi — most innovations land in split ACs first.
  • Long-term ownership: 10-15 year lifespan means lower cost per year despite higher upfront.

Frequently asked questions

Why are window ACs becoming less common?

Modern apartments rarely have the required window cutout; builders design for splits. Split ACs are also quieter and more energy-efficient. Window sales are still strong in rentals and older homes — just not new construction.

Is window AC really louder than split?

Yes, significantly. Window AC has the compressor in the room; split puts compressor outside. Expect 50-60 dB on window vs 32-40 dB on split — noticeable when sleeping or watching TV.

Can split AC indoor and outdoor units be far apart?

Standard installation covers 3-4 metres of copper piping. Longer runs (up to 10-12 metres) are possible but reduce efficiency and add installation cost (₹500-700 per extra metre of copper).

Window AC installation in apartment — is it allowed?

Most apartments allow window ACs if your building permits them. Some high-rises ban outdoor protrusions including window ACs for aesthetic uniformity — check your housing society's rules.

Should I spend more on 5-star split or 3-star window?

5-star split uses 30-40% less electricity than 3-star window, plus runs quieter and lasts longer. The ₹10,000-14,000 price premium pays back in 3-4 years at 8hr/day use. Prefer 5-star split if you own the home.
