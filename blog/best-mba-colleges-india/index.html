How to Read MBA Rankings

Rankings change, but the gap between Tier 1 and Tier 2 does not. Focus on placement quality (median, not max), alumni network, and 3-year ROI.

Tier 1 (Top 10)

1. IIM Ahmedabad

2-year fee: Rs 27 L. Avg placement (2024): Rs 34 LPA. ROI: fee recovered in ~10 months. Best alumni network in India.

2. IIM Bangalore

Fee: Rs 26 L. Avg: Rs 35 LPA. Bengaluru location = tech and startup pipeline.

3. IIM Calcutta

Fee: Rs 27 L. Avg: Rs 35 LPA. Strong finance recruiting.

4. IIM Lucknow

Fee: Rs 21 L. Avg: Rs 32 LPA. Best FMCG recruiting among new IIMs.

5. IIM Indore

Fee: Rs 25 L. Avg: Rs 30 LPA. 5-year IPM programme is a separate track.

6. XLRI Jamshedpur

Fee: Rs 30 L. Avg: Rs 33 LPA. HR specialisation is the best in India.

7. FMS Delhi

Fee: Rs 2.4 L total (yes, two lakh four). Avg: Rs 33 LPA. Best ROI in Indian MBA, full stop.

8. IIM Kozhikode

Fee: Rs 22 L. Avg: Rs 30 LPA. Good consulting recruiting.

9. SPJIMR Mumbai

Fee: Rs 21 L. Avg: Rs 32 LPA. Strong marketing finishing school.

10. MDI Gurgaon

Fee: Rs 25 L. Avg: Rs 28 LPA. NCR corporate access.

Tier 2 Worth Considering

  • IIM Shillong, Trichy, Raipur, Udaipur, Rohtak - fees Rs 15-20 L, avg Rs 18-25 LPA.
  • IIFT Delhi - trade/import-export focus, Rs 22 L fee, Rs 26 LPA avg.
  • NMIMS Mumbai - Rs 22 L fee, Rs 24 LPA avg.
  • IMT Ghaziabad - Rs 21 L fee, Rs 18 LPA avg.
  • TAPMI Manipal - Rs 17 L fee, Rs 17 LPA avg.

ISB Hyderabad - A Separate Category

1-year PGP, fee Rs 48 L, avg Rs 38 LPA. GMAT-based. For those with 2-5 years work ex. Different from 2-year IIM path.

ROI Rankings

  1. FMS Delhi - unmatched, fee-to-salary ratio
  2. IIM A/B/C - premium fee, premium salary
  3. JBIMS Mumbai - Rs 6 L fee, Rs 27 LPA avg

Verdict

If you get IIM A/B/C or FMS, attend. If you get IIM L/I/K or XLRI/SPJIMR, attend. Below that, compare against cost and specialisation. Avoid any college asking Rs 20 L+ where average placements are under Rs 15 LPA - it is not worth it.

Frequently asked questions

Is an MBA worth it in 2025?

From top 15 colleges, yes - ROI is typically positive within 3 years. Below that, only if you need a career switch.

IIM A/B/C - which is best?

All roughly equivalent for placements. Ahmedabad for consulting, Bangalore for tech and start-ups, Calcutta for finance.

Is FMS really Rs 2 lakh only?

Yes, DU-subsidised. It is the best ROI MBA in India. Admission is fiercely competitive via CAT.

ISB or IIM?

ISB if you have 3+ years work ex and want a 1-year programme. IIM if you have 0-2 years and want a 2-year deep immersion.
