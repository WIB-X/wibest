How to Choose a Cancer Hospital

Three things matter: volume (how many cases of your specific cancer per year), multi-disciplinary tumour boards, and access to latest therapies (immunotherapy, proton beam, CAR-T). Brand alone is not enough.

Top Public / Trust Hospitals

1. Tata Memorial Hospital, Mumbai

The benchmark. 70,000+ new cases/year. Subsidised treatment. Strong in all cancer types. Long waiting for new registrations; urgent cases are fast-tracked.

2. AIIMS Delhi (NCI Jhajjar)

National Cancer Institute campus. Subsidised. Strong paediatric oncology.

3. Cancer Institute (WIA), Adyar, Chennai

Trust hospital. Excellent head and neck cancer program. Subsidised.

4. Kidwai Memorial, Bengaluru

State cancer centre. High-volume, affordable.

5. Chittaranjan National Cancer Institute, Kolkata

Government. Strong radiation oncology.

Top Private Hospitals

1. Apollo Cancer Centres (Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Bengaluru)

Proton beam therapy (Chennai), CAR-T programme. Comprehensive.

2. HCG (HealthCare Global)

15+ dedicated cancer hospitals, pan-India. Strong in gynaec-oncology and haematology.

3. Rajiv Gandhi Cancer Institute, Delhi

Trust + private mix. High-volume, excellent BMT programme.

4. Artemis, Gurugram

Strong robotic surgery oncology.

5. Medanta, Gurugram

Multidisciplinary, strong GI oncology.

6. Fortis Memorial Research Institute, Gurugram

Strong haematology-oncology, CyberKnife.

Cost Range (Indicative)

  • Chemotherapy per cycle: Rs 25,000 (public) to Rs 2,00,000 (private, newer drugs)
  • Radiotherapy course: Rs 50,000 (public) to Rs 4,00,000 (private CyberKnife/Proton)
  • Surgery: Rs 50,000 (public) to Rs 8,00,000 (private robotic)
  • CAR-T: Rs 40 L+ (Indian first-in-class approval 2023)

Insurance and Schemes

  • Ayushman Bharat covers Rs 5 L/year at empanelled hospitals.
  • PM-JAY covers most common cancers.
  • Private insurance: confirm cancer sub-limit, co-pay, and day-care exclusion carefully.

What to Ask Before Starting Treatment

  • How many cases of my exact cancer does your team do/year?
  • Is my case discussed at a multi-disciplinary tumour board?
  • Am I eligible for targeted therapy or immunotherapy?
  • What are realistic outcomes at this stage?
  • Do I qualify for clinical trials?

Second Opinion

Always get one, especially before surgery. Tata Memorial offers online second opinions (Rs 3,500-10,000). It is often life-changing.

Verdict

For affordability + quality, Tata Memorial is unmatched. For speed and comfort, Apollo, HCG, Rajiv Gandhi. For rare cancers, tumour-board-driven centres (Tata, AIIMS, Medanta). Always seek a second opinion from a high-volume centre before starting treatment.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tata Memorial really the best?

For outcomes, volume, and research, yes. But waiting times for non-urgent cases can be long. Use Tata for consultation and staging even if you treat elsewhere.

Private vs government cancer hospital?

Quality is comparable at top centres. Cost differs 5-10x. Ayushman Bharat and state schemes can bridge private access for eligible patients.

Is proton beam available in India?

Yes, at Apollo Proton Cancer Centre, Chennai. The only one in South Asia as of June 2025.

How do I take a second opinion?

Most major hospitals (Tata Memorial, Medanta, Apollo) offer teleconsult or online review of reports. Cost Rs 3,000-10,000.

Are stage 4 cancers treatable in India?

Many stage 4 cancers today are manageable with immunotherapy, targeted therapy, or CAR-T. Ask specifically about molecular profiling of your tumour.
