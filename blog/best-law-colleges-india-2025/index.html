Best Law Colleges in India 2025

The legal profession in India is undergoing a transformation — corporate law firms, LPOs (legal process outsourcing), and in-house counsel roles at MNCs are paying ₹12–25 LPA starting salaries at top NLUs. However, the gap between top-10 and other law colleges remains enormous.

National Law Universities (NLUs) — CLAT Ranking

NLSIU Bengaluru (National Law School) — India's undisputed #1. CLAT cut-off 2024: AIR 1–50 (General). Average placement: ₹18–22 LPA. Top recruiters: AZB & Partners, Cyril Amarchand Mangaldas, Trilegal, S&R Associates. Best for corporate/M&A law. Fees: ₹3.5 lakh/year.

NALSAR Hyderabad — #2, equally prestigious for IP and corporate law. CLAT cut-off: AIR 50–150. Average placement: ₹16 LPA. Best for intellectual property law. Fees: ₹3.2 lakh/year.

NUJS Kolkata (National University of Juridical Sciences) — #3, finance-sector law strength. Close to Kolkata's financial district. CLAT: AIR 150–300. Average: ₹15 LPA. Best for banking and finance law.

NLU Delhi — Uses its own entrance (AILET, now merged into CLAT). Smallest intake (70 seats). Supreme Court of India proximity. CLAT: AIR 300–600. Average: ₹14 LPA.

NLU Jodhpur, GNLU Gandhinagar, RGNUL Patiala — Tier 2 NLUs. CLAT: AIR 600–1,500. Average: ₹8–12 LPA. Strong for state bar council careers and government law.

Top Non-NLU Law Colleges

Faculty of Law, Delhi University (Campus Law Centre) — 3-year LLB (after graduation). Cheapest route to a good law degree — fees ₹12,000/year. Strong alumni in Supreme Court and High Courts. Best for those choosing litigation careers.

Government Law College, Mumbai — India's oldest law college (1855). Strong for litigation and Mumbai High Court practice. Fees: ₹5,000/year.

Symbiosis Law School, Pune — Best private law school for corporate law and LPO work. SLAT entrance exam. Fees: ₹3 lakh/year. Average placement: ₹7 LPA.

Jindal Global Law School, Sonepat — India's most internationally oriented private law school. US/UK law school comparison-style curriculum. Average: ₹9 LPA. Fees: ₹5 lakh/year. LLM options abroad facilitated.

5-Year Integrated LLB vs 3-Year LLB

5-year BA/BBA/BSc LLB (after 12th): Starts at NLUs/Symbiosis. Better NLU brand, complete law firm recruiting, built-in internships from Year 3. Recommended for those certain about law career.

3-year LLB (after graduation): Delhi University, Government Law College. Better if you want to keep options open or already have a degree. Cost-effective. Strong for litigation careers.

Frequently asked questions

What CLAT score is needed for NLSIU Bengaluru?

NLSIU typically requires an All India Rank of 1–50 for General category in the CLAT. The actual score varies by difficulty of the paper — in 2024, this corresponded to approximately 108–114 out of 120 marks.

What is the average salary after NLU?

Top NLUs (NLSIU, NALSAR, NUJS) place students at ₹15–25 LPA at Tier 1 law firms in their final year. Corporate/M&A associates at AZB, Cyril Amarchand, or Trilegal start at ₹12–18 LPA and reach ₹30–50 LPA as senior associates within 5 years.

Is law a good career in India 2025?

Law has two very different career paths: corporate law (NLU graduates at top firms, earning ₹15–50 LPA) and litigation (High Court/District Court practice, highly variable income but respected career). Corporate law has seen salary growth of 20–30% per year at top firms in 2023–25.
