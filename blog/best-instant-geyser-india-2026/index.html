Instant vs Storage — Which Geyser Do You Need?

Instant (3-10 L): Heats water on-demand as it flows through. Compact, low electricity for short use, but limited flow rate. Best for kitchens and guest bathrooms.

Storage (10-25 L): Heats and stores water. Higher capacity, better for hot water showers by multiple people, but uses more electricity due to standby heat loss.

This post covers instant geysers — ideal for kitchens, small bathrooms, and homes with one-person-at-a-time bathing.

Tank Material — Glass-Lined vs Copper vs Stainless Steel

  • Glass-lined: Best corrosion resistance, longest warranty (7-10 years). AO Smith, Havells, Crompton top models.
  • Copper tank (SS outside): Good for hard water, but heavier. Racold, some V-Guard.
  • Stainless steel only: Budget option. Rusts in hard water areas over 5-6 years. Bajaj, Orient, entry-level models.

Safety Features to Demand

  • ISI mark (BIS certified): Mandatory. Ensures pressure-release valve and earthing comply.
  • Multi-functional safety valve: Releases pressure if thermostat fails. Every model on this list has this.
  • Shock Safe / fuseless plug: Ensures no current leaks through wet skin. Havells and Crompton are strong here.
  • Dry-heating prevention: Cuts off if no water (prevents element damage). Auto-resets.

Installation Tips

Get a licensed electrician — geysers draw 13-amp load. Check your bathroom's electrical rating first; older homes may have only 5-amp sockets. Installation costs ₹400-700 including pipe fittings. For high-rise apartments above 4th floor, check water pressure — some instant geysers need minimum 0.2 bar.

Frequently asked questions

Instant vs storage geyser — which saves more electricity?

Instant wins for short uses (hand wash, face wash, dish wash) — no standby loss. Storage wins for showers by multiple people — one heating cycle serves many baths. Pick based on usage pattern, not generic advice.

Is 3L enough for a full shower?

No. 3L provides warm water for about 90 seconds of flow. Fine for buckets and short rinses. For full showers, you need a 15-25L storage geyser or 7L+ instant.

Why does my geyser's element fail after 2-3 years?

Almost always due to hard water (TDS >300 ppm) causing scale buildup. Solution: install a water softener or choose a model with incoloy element (resistant to scaling) — Havells, AO Smith premium.

Can I leave the geyser switch ON always?

Not advisable. Modern geysers cut off at set temperature, so power use is minimal, but constant heating stresses the tank. Turn on 10-15 minutes before use for instant; ON-once-per-day for storage.

Glass-lined vs copper tank — which is better for hard water?

Glass-lined (porcelain enamel) coating is the best defence against hard-water corrosion. Copper tanks are OK but heavy. Pure stainless steel rusts fastest in high-TDS water. Prefer glass-lined with 7+ year warranty in hard-water cities.
