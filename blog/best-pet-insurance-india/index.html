Why Pet Insurance Matters in 2025

Vet costs in metros have doubled since 2020. A TPLO knee surgery for a Labrador costs ₹50,000-1.2 lakh. Cancer treatment can run into ₹2-3 lakh. With 3+ crore pets in Indian homes, pet insurance is finally getting traction.

Bajaj Allianz Pet Dog Insurance — Most Popular

Best in class with 3rd party liability cover (pays if your dog bites someone). Optional add-ons: long-distance transportation, advertisement cost (for missing dog), terminal disease cover. Available for dogs aged 3 months to 8 years.

Future Generali — Best Coverage

Up to ₹2 lakh sum insured. Covers hospitalization, surgery, daycare procedures. Vaccination must be up-to-date for claims. Also covers cats (most insurers cover dogs only).

New India Assurance — PSU Trust

Government-backed insurer. Affordable but limited coverage. Mortality and accident-only plans available. Best for first-time buyers wanting basic protection.

Pawtect — Pet-Specific Premium

Pet-only insurance startup. Higher premiums but covers OPD visits, vaccinations, deworming. Wellness plans available alongside health insurance.

What's Typically Covered

  • Surgery and hospitalization
  • Accidental injury
  • Illness (after waiting period of 30-90 days)
  • Third-party liability (dog bite, property damage)
  • Death due to disease/accident (mortality cover)
  • Theft of purebred (with FIR)

What's Not Covered

  • Pre-existing conditions
  • Pregnancy/whelping (usually)
  • Cosmetic surgery
  • Cost of feeding/grooming
  • Dogs older than 8 years (most plans)
  • Vaccinations (unless OPD add-on)

Tips

  • Insure when pup is young (3-12 months) to lock low premiums.
  • Pedigree certificate may be needed for purebred.
  • Health certificate from vet at policy start (for older pets).
  • Most plans have waiting period of 30-90 days for illness.
  • Microchipping is mandatory for some insurers.

Frequently asked questions

Is pet insurance worth it in India?

Yes if your pet is a purebred or you can't afford ₹1-2 lakh emergency vet bills. Premium of ₹2-5K/year is small protection.

What pets can be insured in India?

Mostly dogs. A few insurers (Future Generali, Pawtect) cover cats. Exotic pets and birds typically not covered.

What's the maximum pet insurance cover?

Up to ₹3 lakh via Pawtect plans. Mainstream insurers like Bajaj Allianz cap at ₹1.5 lakh.

Are vaccinations covered?

Not in standard plans. OPD add-ons or wellness plans (Pawtect) cover vaccines, deworming, and preventive care.
