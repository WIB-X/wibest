Why You Need ₹10 Lakh+ Cover in 2025

A typical heart bypass in a Tier-1 metro hospital now costs ₹4-6 lakh. Cancer treatment can run into ₹15-25 lakh. Health insurance is no longer optional — it's essential financial protection. Family floater plans cover spouse, kids, and sometimes parents under one sum insured.

HDFC ERGO Optima Secure — Best Overall

The Secure Benefit doubles your sum insured from day one (₹10L becomes ₹20L). Plus Restore Benefit refills 100% of sum insured if exhausted. Plus Benefit gives 50% bonus every claim-free year. Effectively a ₹10L plan acts like ₹40-50L over time.

Niva Bupa ReAssure 2.0 — Best Restoration

Unlimited restoration means even if you exhaust your cover 5 times in a year, it gets refilled. Lock the Clock feature freezes your age for premium calculation until you make a claim — huge savings for healthy families.

Star Family Health Optima — Best for South India

Star has the largest cashless network in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra and Telangana. Newborn cover from day 1, automatic restoration, and recharge benefit. Most affordable in this list.

What to Look For

  • No room rent capping: Avoid plans with 1% sub-limit. HDFC ERGO and Niva Bupa have no capping.
  • No co-payment: Especially for senior citizens.
  • Pre-existing disease (PED) waiting: 2-3 years is industry standard.
  • Daycare procedures: 500+ procedures should be covered.
  • Network hospitals: Check if your preferred hospital is in the cashless list.

Add-Ons Worth Buying

OPD cover (₹5,000-15,000/yr), maternity cover (waiting period 2-4 years), critical illness rider (₹10-25L), and personal accident cover. Skip non-medical expense cover — premium hike isn't worth it.

Frequently asked questions

What sum insured is enough for family of 4?

Minimum ₹10 lakh in metros, ₹5 lakh in Tier-2 cities. Add a super top-up of ₹25-50 lakh for catastrophic illness coverage.

Should I buy from employer or separate plan?

Always buy a separate plan. Employer cover ends when you switch jobs and rarely covers parents adequately.

What is restoration benefit?

If you use up your sum insured, the insurer refills it for unrelated illnesses in the same policy year. Look for 100% or unlimited restoration.

Are pre-existing diseases covered?

Yes, but only after a waiting period of 2-4 years depending on plan. Niva Bupa offers 2-year PED waiting which is among the best.
