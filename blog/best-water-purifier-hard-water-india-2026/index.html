Why Hard Water Is a Problem in Indian Homes

Most Indian cities have TDS (total dissolved solids) between 300-1,500 ppm. WHO recommends below 500 ppm for drinking. Hard water causes kidney stones, taste issues, and destroys washing machines, geysers, and pipes. RO is the only technology that reliably reduces TDS.

Which Technology Do You Need?

  • TDS above 500 ppm (most Indian cities): RO is mandatory. UV+UF alone won't reduce TDS.
  • TDS 200-500 ppm (some boreholes, Mumbai supply): UV+UF is enough; RO may strip too many minerals.
  • TDS under 200 ppm (soft water areas): UV+UF only; RO wastes water unnecessarily.
  • Municipal water from treatment plants: Always add UV (kills bacteria and viruses from pipe contamination).

Hidden Costs

RO water purifiers reject 3 litres of water for every 1 litre filtered — check waste-water ratio. Newer models save this water for mopping/flushing. Annual filter replacement: ₹3,000-5,500 depending on brand. AMC (annual maintenance contract): ₹1,500-3,500/year extra.

Installation and Placement

Wall-mount or countertop. Needs access to water supply (under sink or from tap) and a 15-amp power socket. Filter replacement typically needs a technician — DIY is possible but cartridges are proprietary. Installation charge: ₹300-500.

Frequently asked questions

What TDS level is safe for drinking?

WHO recommends 50-150 ppm for ideal taste and mineral content. Below 50 ppm is too demineralised; above 500 ppm may cause kidney stones long-term.

Is RO water bad because it removes minerals?

Pure RO water removes useful minerals (calcium, magnesium). Modern RO purifiers with TDS controllers or mineralisers add these back. If yours doesn't, compensate with a varied diet — but most Indian diets already provide adequate minerals.

Why does RO reject so much water?

RO membranes need cross-flow water pressure to work — typically 3 litres in, 1 litre out. New 'zero water waste' technology (found in some premium models) reuses reject water. Most mid-range RO models still waste 60-70%.

How often should I replace RO filters?

Pre-filter (sediment/carbon): every 6-8 months. RO membrane: every 18-24 months. UV lamp: every 12-15 months. Annual filter change costs ₹3,000-5,500 depending on brand.

Can I use RO water for plants and pets?

Yes, but plants actually do better with regular tap water (minerals). Pets can drink RO water safely but the same applies — they benefit from mineral content in regular water. Use RO for drinking and cooking only.
