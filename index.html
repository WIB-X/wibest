Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

WIB ("we", "our", or "us") operates wibest.in. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and referring URL — collected automatically via Google Analytics.
  • Review submissions: Name, email address, and review content submitted voluntarily through our review forms.
  • Contact form data: Name, email, and message submitted through our contact page.
  • Cookies: We use cookies for analytics and to remember your preferences. No personally identifiable cookies are set without your knowledge.

2. How we use your information

  • To display community reviews on school, college, and product pages.
  • To respond to contact and enquiry submissions.
  • To understand how visitors use our site and improve the experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-party services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Analytics: Tracks anonymous usage data. You can opt out via Google's opt-out tool.
  • Firebase (Google): Used to store listing data and community reviews.
  • Amazon Associates: We participate in the Amazon Associates affiliate programme. Clicking Amazon links on our site may place affiliate cookies. We earn a small commission on qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you.
  • Practo: Hospitals page links to Practo for appointment booking. Practo has its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): Anonymous, help us understand traffic patterns.
  • Affiliate cookies (Amazon): Set when you click an Amazon link from our site.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

5. Data retention

Review and contact form data is retained as long as it is relevant to our service. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics' default retention period (26 months). You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for review submissions.

To exercise these rights, email us at hello@wibest.in.

7. Children's privacy

WIB is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at hello@wibest.in or through our contact page.
