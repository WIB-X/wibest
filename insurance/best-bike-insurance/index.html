Frequently asked questions

Is bike insurance mandatory in India?

Third-party bike insurance is mandatory by law (Motor Vehicles Act 1988). Riding without it can result in Rs 2,000 fine for first offence + Rs 4,000 + 3-month jail for repeat offence. Comprehensive cover is optional but strongly recommended for any bike worth over Rs 50,000.

How much does bike insurance cost in India?

Third-party only: Rs 700-1,500/year depending on engine cc. Comprehensive (own damage + third party): Rs 1,500-3,500/year for typical commuter bikes (Hero Splendor to Bajaj Pulsar). Premium bikes (RE Classic, KTM RC) cost Rs 3,500-7,000/year for full cover.

Can I renew bike insurance online?

Yes — all major insurers offer online renewal in 5-10 minutes. Policybazaar, InsuranceDekho, and Coverfox aggregators let you compare 10+ insurers in one place. Online renewal is typically 10-15% cheaper than agent-renewed policies.

What does zero depreciation cover for bikes?

Zero depreciation pays full part-replacement cost without deducting depreciation. For bike plastic parts (fairing, tank cover), depreciation otherwise reduces payout by 50%. A Rs 8,000 fairing replacement becomes Rs 4,000 covered without zero dep. Worth the Rs 200-500/year extra premium.

Is bike insurance valid for delivery / commercial use?

No — personal bike insurance is invalid if used for commercial delivery (Swiggy, Zomato, Dunzo). You need commercial vehicle insurance, which costs 30-50% more. Using personal insurance for commercial purposes leads to claim rejection. If you do delivery work, declare it and buy commercial cover.
