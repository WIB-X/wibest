Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of angioplasty in Mumbai?

Angioplasty in Mumbai ranges from Rs 1.5-3 lakh at private hospitals to Rs 30,000-60,000 at government hospitals. Stent type (drug-eluting vs bare-metal) and number of stents heavily influence final cost. Most insurance plans cover angioplasty with prior authorization.

Which hospital is best for heart bypass surgery in Mumbai?

The top-rated cardiology hospitals in Mumbai are listed above, ranked by patient outcomes, surgeon experience, and CTVS infrastructure. Always verify the operating cardiac surgeon's case history and the hospital's post-op ICU capability before deciding.

Do these hospitals accept Ayushman Bharat for cardiology?

Government and select private hospitals in Mumbai accept Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) for cardiology procedures. Coverage caps apply per procedure. Always confirm with the hospital's billing desk and your local PMJAY office before admission.

What is the success rate for angioplasty in Mumbai?

Established cardiac centres in Mumbai report 95-99% procedural success rates for elective angioplasty. Emergency angioplasty (during a heart attack) success depends on time-to-balloon — under 90 minutes from arrival is the global benchmark these hospitals aim for.
