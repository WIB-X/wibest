Frequently asked questions

Which is the best cancer hospital in Mumbai?

The top oncology hospitals in Mumbai are ranked above based on tumour board composition, radiation infrastructure, and dedicated onco-pathology services. Tata Memorial network, HCG, and major corporate hospitals lead most Indian cities.

What is the cost of cancer treatment in Mumbai?

Cancer treatment cost in Mumbai varies widely — chemotherapy can range from Rs 50,000 to Rs 5 lakh per cycle depending on drug regimen. Radiation (28 sittings) costs Rs 1.5-4 lakh. Major surgery starts at Rs 1.5 lakh. Insurance pre-authorization is critical.

Do these hospitals offer second-opinion services?

Most major oncology centres in Mumbai offer second-opinion consultations, often via teleconsultation. Tata Memorial Mumbai is the gold standard for second opinions and accepts referrals nationally.

Are these cancer hospitals NABH-accredited?

Yes — all listed hospitals carry NABH accreditation as a minimum. Top oncology centres often hold additional QCI/ICMR-recognised certifications for clinical trials and bone marrow transplant programmes.
