Cancer treatment cost in Lucknow varies — chemotherapy Rs 50,000-5 lakh per cycle. Radiation (28 sittings) Rs 1.5-4 lakh. Major surgery starts at Rs 1.5 lakh. Insurance pre-authorization is critical.
