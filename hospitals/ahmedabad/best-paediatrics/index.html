Frequently asked questions

Which hospital has the best NICU in Ahmedabad?

The top paediatric hospitals in Ahmedabad run Level III NICUs with neonatologist coverage 24x7. NICUs with high preterm survival rates (below 28 weeks gestation) are typically NABH-accredited and recognised by national paediatric bodies.

What is the cost of paediatric surgery in Ahmedabad?

Paediatric surgery cost varies hugely — minor procedures from Rs 30,000, complex cardiac or oncology surgery from Rs 2-8 lakh. Most paediatric departments offer treatment plans with insurance coordination upfront.

Do these hospitals have child-friendly facilities?

Most modern paediatric departments in Ahmedabad have play areas, child-friendly room decor, parent-stay facilities, and trained child-life specialists. Always tour the paediatric wing before scheduled admissions.

When to take a child to the emergency room?

Take a child to ER for: difficulty breathing, persistent high fever (>104°F), unconsciousness, severe vomiting or dehydration, head injury with vomiting/lethargy, or any sudden onset weakness. Most listed hospitals run 24x7 paediatric emergency.
