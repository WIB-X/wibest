Chennai is widely considered India's healthcare capital, with Apollo Hospitals founded here in 1983. Sankara Nethralaya is one of the world's leading eye-care institutions, and Madras Medical Mission is internationally recognised for paediatric cardiac surgery. Greams Road, Mogappair, Porur and Adyar are the main hospital corridors.
