Frequently asked questions

Which is the best hospital for stroke in Pune?

Stroke outcomes depend on speed — the best stroke hospitals in Pune run 24x7 stroke units with door-to-thrombolysis under 60 minutes. The hospitals listed above are ranked by stroke care infrastructure and outcomes.

What is the cost of brain surgery in Pune?

Brain surgery in Pune ranges from Rs 1.5-7 lakh depending on procedure complexity. Aneurysm clipping, tumour removal, and minimally-invasive endoscopic procedures all have different cost brackets. Insurance pre-authorization is essential.

Do these hospitals treat epilepsy?

Major neurology centres in Pune have epilepsy clinics with 24-hour video-EEG monitoring. Surgical epilepsy programs are available at top centres. Always verify the neurologist's sub-specialty expertise before referral.

How do I find the best neurosurgeon in Pune?

Look for neurosurgeons with sub-specialty fellowship training (vascular, spine, oncology). The hospitals listed above employ multiple sub-specialists. Cross-verify with peer-reviewed publications and case load — high-volume centres typically have better outcomes.
