Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of knee replacement in Delhi?

Total knee replacement in Delhi costs Rs 1.5-4 lakh per knee at private hospitals. Implant choice (standard vs high-flex vs robotic-assisted) is the biggest cost driver. Most health insurance plans cover knee replacement with adequate sum insured.

Which hospital is best for joint replacement in Delhi?

The top-ranked orthopaedic hospitals in Delhi above offer high-volume joint replacement programs. Look for centres performing 500+ joints/year — high volume correlates strongly with better outcomes and shorter hospital stay.

Do these hospitals offer minimally-invasive spine surgery?

Yes — leading orthopaedic centres in Delhi offer minimally invasive spine surgery (MISS) including endoscopic discectomy and percutaneous fixation. Recovery time is significantly shorter than open spine procedures.

What is the best hospital for sports injuries in Delhi?

For sports injuries, look for hospitals with dedicated sports medicine departments offering arthroscopy and ACL reconstruction. Several centres listed above have sports orthopaedic specialists who work with professional athletes.
