Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of delivery in Delhi?

Normal delivery in Delhi costs Rs 30,000-1.5 lakh at private hospitals. C-section ranges from Rs 60,000-2.5 lakh. Premium maternity packages with private rooms cost more. Most maternity insurance plans have separate sub-limits.

Which hospital is best for IVF in Delhi?

The top IVF centres in Delhi have high cycle volumes and transparent success rate publishing (per ESHRE format). IVF success depends heavily on patient age and cause of infertility — always discuss expected outcomes for your case.

Do these hospitals offer painless delivery?

Most major maternity hospitals in Delhi offer painless delivery (epidural). Confirm 24x7 anaesthetist availability before booking — epidural requires immediate anaesthetist presence which not all hospitals guarantee at night.

Are these hospitals breastfeeding-friendly?

Many leading maternity centres in Delhi are BFHI (Baby-Friendly Hospital Initiative) certified or follow similar protocols. They provide lactation consultants, rooming-in, and avoid formula promotion. Ask about BFHI status before booking.
