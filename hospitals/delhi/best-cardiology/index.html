Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of angioplasty in Delhi?

Angioplasty in Delhi ranges from Rs 1.5-3 lakh at private hospitals to Rs 30,000-60,000 at government hospitals. Stent type (drug-eluting vs bare-metal) and number of stents heavily influence final cost. Most insurance plans cover angioplasty with prior authorization.

Which hospital is best for heart bypass surgery in Delhi?

The top-rated cardiology hospitals in Delhi are listed above, ranked by patient outcomes, surgeon experience, and CTVS infrastructure. Always verify the operating cardiac surgeon's case history and the hospital's post-op ICU capability before deciding.

Do these hospitals accept Ayushman Bharat for cardiology?

Government and select private hospitals in Delhi accept Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) for cardiology procedures. Coverage caps apply per procedure. Always confirm with the hospital's billing desk and your local PMJAY office before admission.

What is the success rate for angioplasty in Delhi?

Established cardiac centres in Delhi report 95-99% procedural success rates for elective angioplasty. Emergency angioplasty (during a heart attack) success depends on time-to-balloon — under 90 minutes from arrival is the global benchmark these hospitals aim for.
