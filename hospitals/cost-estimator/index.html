📋 About these estimates

Cost data sourced from: Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS) rate lists, NHA (National Health Authority) package rates, and published hospital tariff cards. Metropolitan costs are 20–35% higher than Tier 2 cities. Emergency admissions add 20–40% to planned procedure costs due to ICU charges and extended stays. Senior patients (60+) typically have 15–25% higher costs due to longer recovery and additional monitoring. Comorbidities add 10–30% to base procedure costs.
