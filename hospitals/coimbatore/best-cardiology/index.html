Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of angioplasty in Coimbatore?

Angioplasty in Coimbatore ranges from Rs 1.5-3 lakh at private hospitals to Rs 30,000-60,000 at government hospitals. Stent type and number heavily influence final cost. Most insurance plans cover angioplasty with prior authorization.

Which hospital is best for heart bypass surgery in Coimbatore?

The top-rated cardiology hospitals in Coimbatore are listed above, ranked by patient outcomes, surgeon experience, and CTVS infrastructure. Verify the cardiac surgeon's case history and ICU capability before deciding.

Do these hospitals accept Ayushman Bharat for cardiology?

Government and select private hospitals in Coimbatore accept Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY) for cardiology procedures. Always confirm with the hospital's billing desk before admission.

What is the success rate for angioplasty in Coimbatore?

Established cardiac centres in Coimbatore report 95-99% procedural success rates for elective angioplasty. Emergency angioplasty success depends on time-to-balloon — under 90 minutes is the global benchmark.
