How to choose the right phone in India (2026)

With new launches every month from Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Nothing, iQOO, Realme and Xiaomi, picking a phone is overwhelming. The quiz above narrows it to one model, but here is what actually separates a good buy from a regret.

What actually matters

  • Set the budget band first. Sub-Rs15k, Rs15-30k, Rs30-60k and Rs60k+ are genuinely different markets. Decide the band, then optimise within it; do not stretch for one spec.
  • Processor and update years. A Snapdragon 7-series or Dimensity 8000-class chip with 4+ years of OS updates ages far better than a slightly bigger battery.
  • Camera vs gaming vs battery. No phone wins all three at once. Rank your top use, because the trade-off is real below Rs40k.
  • Service network in your city. Apple and Samsung have the widest service reach; some brands are thin in Tier-2/3 towns. Check before you buy.

Common questions

What is the best phone under Rs20,000 in India?

In this band, prioritise a clean software experience, a Dimensity or Snapdragon mid chip, and a 5,000mAh battery over a high megapixel count. Run the quiz for a model matched to your specific use.

Choose iPhone for long update support and resale value; Android for charging speed, customisation, and more spec per rupee. Your priorities in the quiz decide which fits.
