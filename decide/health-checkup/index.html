How to choose a health checkup package in India (2026)

Full-body packages range from Rs999 to Rs15,000 and most people over-test or under-test. The quiz matches a package to your age and risk; here is what matters.

What actually matters

  • Match tests to age and history. Under 30 needs basics; 40+ should add cardiac, diabetes and cancer markers relevant to family history.
  • Lab accreditation. Choose NABL-accredited labs (Apollo, Metropolis, Thyrocare, Redcliffe, Tata 1mg) for reliable results.
  • Home collection and report quality. Free home sample collection and a doctor-reviewed report add real value over a bare test list.
  • Do not over-test. More tests are not better; incidental findings cause anxiety and needless follow-ups. Target what is clinically useful for you.

Common questions

How often should I get a full-body checkup?

Once a year is reasonable for most adults over 30, or more often with chronic conditions or a strong family history. Under 30 and healthy, every 2-3 years is usually enough.

Which is the best full-body checkup in India?

The best depends on your age, gender and risk profile, not the test count. Pick an NABL-accredited lab and a package matched to your needs; the quiz helps narrow it.
