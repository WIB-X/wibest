    How to Decide Where to Live in India

    Choosing a city to live in India comes down to five trade-offs: career opportunities, cost of living, climate, infrastructure, and proximity to family. This 90-second quiz weighs your priorities across these factors and matches you against WIB's City Living Index data for 18 Indian cities — covering rent, commute times, air quality, healthcare access, school quality, and job market depth.

    What the quiz considers

    • Career: IT hubs (Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune), finance/media (Mumbai), manufacturing (Chennai, Ahmedabad), government (Delhi)
    • Budget: Monthly costs range from ₹25,000 (Ahmedabad, Indore) to ₹1,00,000+ (Mumbai) for a couple
    • Climate: Year-round pleasant (Pune, Bangalore) vs hot summers (Hyderabad, Delhi) vs humid coasts (Mumbai, Chennai)
    • Lifestyle: Nightlife and dining depth, walkability, parks, and cultural scene
