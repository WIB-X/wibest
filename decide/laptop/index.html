How to choose a laptop in India (2026)

Students, creators and work-from-home professionals need very different machines. The quiz matches you to one; these are the factors that matter most in India right now.

What actually matters

  • CPU class over raw GHz. An Intel Core 5/7 (2024+), AMD Ryzen 5/7 or Apple M-series handles most work for years. Do not overpay for flagship chips you will not load.
  • RAM and SSD are the real bottleneck. 16GB RAM with a 512GB SSD is the 2026 sweet spot for longevity; 8GB is now entry-only.
  • Battery and weight for portability. If you commute, sub-1.5kg and 10h+ battery matter more than a discrete GPU.
  • Warranty and service. On-site warranty and an in-city service centre save weeks of downtime; check coverage for your pincode.

Common questions

Is a MacBook Air worth it for students in India?

The M-series Air offers excellent battery and resale but limited gaming and pricier repairs. For coding, writing and design it is strong; for gaming or budget builds, a Windows laptop wins.

How much RAM do I need in 2026?

16GB for general use and multitasking, 32GB for video editing or heavy development. 8GB only for light browsing and documents.
