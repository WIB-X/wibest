How to choose your next holiday destination from India (2026)

Domestic or international, beach or mountains, budget or splurge: the quiz picks a destination for your dates and budget. Here is the framework behind it.

What actually matters

  • Budget and visa effort. Visa-free or visa-on-arrival countries (Thailand, Malaysia, UAE, Sri Lanka, Bali) cut cost and hassle versus Schengen or US.
  • Season over calendar. Match the destination to its best season; monsoon ruins some beaches but makes the Western Ghats stunning.
  • Travel time vs trip length. For a 4-5 day break, keep flights short; save long-haul for 10+ day trips.
  • Forex and local costs. Southeast Asia stretches the rupee far further than Europe; factor daily spend, not just airfare.

Common questions

Best international trip from India on a budget?

Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam, Bali) and the UAE offer easy visas, short flights and rupee-friendly costs. The quiz matches a destination to your exact budget and dates.

When is the cheapest time to travel from India?

Off-peak months and mid-week departures are cheapest; avoid school holidays and long weekends. Booking 6-8 weeks ahead usually gets the best fares.
