How to choose a home loan in India (2026)

A 0.3% rate difference on a Rs50 lakh loan is several lakhs over the tenure. The quiz matches you to a lender; these factors decide the real cost.

What actually matters

  • Compare the spread, not just the headline rate. Rates are repo-linked; what varies is the bank spread and your credit-score-based offer. A 750+ CIBIL unlocks the best slabs.
  • Processing fee and prepayment terms. Floating-rate home loans have no prepayment penalty; use that. Watch one-time processing fees and legal or valuation charges.
  • PSU vs private vs HFC. PSUs (SBI, PNB) are often cheapest and most trusted; private banks faster; housing finance companies flexible for self-employed profiles.
  • Women co-applicant discount. Many lenders offer about 0.05% off for women borrowers or co-owners, plus stamp-duty benefits in some states.

Common questions

Which bank has the lowest home loan rate in India?

Rates move with the RBI repo and your credit profile, so the lowest offer is borrower-specific. SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis and LIC Housing are the usual front-runners; the quiz estimates your best match and EMI.

Fixed or floating home loan rate?

Floating is usually cheaper over a full tenure and allows penalty-free prepayment. Choose fixed only if you specifically need predictable EMIs and accept a higher rate.
