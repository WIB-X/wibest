How to choose a weekend trip in India (2026)

A great weekend getaway is mostly about travel time from your city. The quiz matches one to your base and vibe; here is how to pick well.

What actually matters

  • Keep one-way travel under 5-6 hours. For a 2-day trip, anything further eats the weekend. Prioritise reachable-by-road or a short flight.
  • Match the vibe. Hills, beach, heritage or wildlife: pick one focus rather than cramming.
  • Avoid peak crowding. Popular hill stations choke on long weekends; consider lesser-known alternatives nearby.
  • Book stay first in season. Good weekend stays sell out early in peak months; lock accommodation before transport.
