Frequently asked questions

What can I do after BA in Chennai?

BA opens many paths: MA followed by academia/research, civil services preparation, journalism, content roles, law (after entrance), MBA (after work experience), and increasingly tech roles after upskilling. Chennai has strong corporate ecosystem to absorb arts graduates.

Which arts college in Chennai has the best placements?

Top arts colleges in Chennai place graduates in consulting, content writing, edtech, journalism, and corporate communications. Placement rates depend heavily on individual student preparation, internships, and skills beyond academics — college brand only opens initial doors.

What are arts college fees in Chennai?

Government arts college fees in Chennai range Rs 5,000-25,000 per year. Top private arts colleges (Christ, St. Xaviers, Loyola) charge Rs 50,000-1.5 lakh per year. Liberal arts universities like Ashoka, Krea charge Rs 8-12 lakh per year.

Are liberal arts degrees better than traditional BA?

Liberal arts (multidisciplinary) degrees suit students who want flexibility to combine subjects (e.g., economics + computer science + philosophy). Traditional BA is more focused. Choice depends on career clarity — liberal arts is better for exploration, traditional BA for specialization.
