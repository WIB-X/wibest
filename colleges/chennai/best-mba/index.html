Frequently asked questions

What CAT percentile is required for top MBA colleges in Chennai?

IIM-tier colleges in Chennai need CAT 95+ percentile. Tier-2 MBA colleges and university programs accept 70-85 percentile typically. XAT, GMAT, and CMAT scores are also accepted by many institutions.

What are MBA fees in Chennai?

IIM and top private MBA fees in Chennai range Rs 15-25 lakh for the 2-year program. Mid-tier MBA programs cost Rs 5-12 lakh. State universities offer MBA at Rs 2-4 lakh. Always compare against expected post-MBA salary for ROI.

Which MBA specialization has best placements in Chennai?

Finance, consulting, and product management roles typically command the highest packages from Chennai MBA programs. Marketing and HR have strong demand from FMCG and tech companies. Specialization choice should align with both market demand and personal interest.

Is an MBA worth it in 2026?

MBA value depends on the program tier, your work experience, and career goals. Top-tier MBAs (IIM A/B/C, XLRI, ISB) consistently deliver strong ROI through career switches and higher packages. Tier-3 MBA value is less clear — research alumni outcomes carefully.
