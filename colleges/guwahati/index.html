Best Colleges in Guwahati — Overview

Guwahati is the higher-education capital of Northeast India, anchored by IIT Guwahati — one of India's top engineering institutes — and historic universities that serve students from across all eight Northeastern states.

Top institutions: IIT Guwahati (premier, among India's best), Gauhati University and Cotton University (the historic flagship for arts & sciences), Assam Engineering College, Gauhati Medical College, IIIT Guwahati, and Assam Don Bosco University.

Government institutes offer excellent education at low fees. Key education areas: North Guwahati (IITG), Jalukbari, Panbazar, and Bhangagarh.
