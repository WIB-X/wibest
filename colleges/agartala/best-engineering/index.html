Frequently asked questions

What is the JEE cutoff for top engineering colleges in Agartala?

JEE Main cutoffs vary by college — top NITs and IIITs in Agartala typically need JEE Main percentile above 95+ for general category. State quota and tier-2 colleges accept lower cutoffs from 70-90 percentile depending on stream and category.

What is the average engineering college fee in Agartala?

Government engineering colleges in Agartala (NITs, IITs) charge Rs 1.5-2.5 lakh per year. Private engineering colleges range from Rs 2-7 lakh per year. Top-tier private colleges (BITS, VIT, Manipal-affiliated) can be Rs 5-10 lakh per year.

Which engineering branch has the best placements in Agartala?

Computer Science Engineering (CSE) and AI/ML branches typically have the highest placement averages in Agartala. Core branches like mechanical and civil have improved with infrastructure-sector growth. Top colleges publish branch-wise placement data — always check before choosing.

How important is NAAC/NBA accreditation for engineering colleges?

NAAC and NBA accreditation matter for higher education recognition, scholarship eligibility, and government job applications. NBA program-specific accreditation matters most for engineering — verify each program separately, not just the college overall.
