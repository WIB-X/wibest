OnePlus 13 vs Samsung Galaxy S25

Two flagship Snapdragon 8 Elite phones at very different price points. OnePlus 13 is the value flagship — massive battery, 100W charging, and Hasselblad cameras at Rs 69,999. Samsung Galaxy S25 is the ecosystem flagship — 7 years of updates, Galaxy AI, and Samsung's best-in-class camera processing at Rs 80,999. Here's which to buy in 2026.

Camera

Both phones use the same Snapdragon 8 Elite processor but diverge sharply on camera philosophy. OnePlus 13 uses Hasselblad colour science that produces warmer, more natural tones — excellent for portraits and travel. Samsung S25's ProVisual Engine is more aggressive with sharpening and detail extraction, particularly in low-light situations where it pulls ahead. Video is where Samsung wins decisively: stabilisation, log profile support, and consistent colour grading put it closer to a compact camera than a phone.

Battery & charging

OnePlus 13 has a commanding 6000 mAh battery vs S25's 4000 mAh — in real use that's 1.5 days of heavy use vs a tight single day. The 100W charging means you plug in for 30 minutes and leave with a full phone; S25's 25W takes 65+ minutes. If you're often away from chargers, OnePlus 13 is the clear winner here.

Software & longevity

Samsung's 7-year Android update promise is the best in the Android ecosystem — your S25 will receive updates through Android 21. OnePlus promises 4 OS updates (through Android 18) and 5 years of security patches. If you keep phones for 4+ years, the S25's update longevity alone justifies the premium. Galaxy AI (Circle to Search, Live Translate, Note Assist) is also more mature than OnePlus's AI suite.

The verdict

Buy OnePlus 13 if: you want the best battery life + fastest charging at a lower price, and keep phones for 2-3 years.

Buy Samsung Galaxy S25 if: you keep phones for 4+ years, use Samsung ecosystem (Watch, tablet, laptop), want the best camera consistency, or want maximum resale value.

Frequently asked questions

Is OnePlus 13 better than Samsung S25?

For most Indian buyers, OnePlus 13 offers better value — faster charging (100W vs 25W), larger battery, and comparable Snapdragon 8 Elite performance at Rs 10,000-15,000 less. Samsung S25 wins on software longevity (7 years), ecosystem (Galaxy AI, SmartThings), and resale value.

Which has a better camera — OnePlus 13 or Samsung S25?

Samsung S25 has better consistent camera output especially in low-light, with superior processing from ProVisual Engine. OnePlus 13 with Hasselblad tuning produces more natural colours. For Instagram and casual use, both are excellent. S25 wins for professionals.

How long will Samsung S25 receive software updates?

Samsung S25 is guaranteed 7 years of Android OS updates and 7 years of security patches — the best in the Android world. OnePlus 13 gets 4 years of OS updates and 5 years of security patches.

Which charges faster — OnePlus 13 or Samsung S25?

OnePlus 13 charges dramatically faster — 100W wired charging fills it in ~30 minutes. Samsung S25 supports only 25W wired charging, taking 65-75 minutes for a full charge. OnePlus wins on charging by a huge margin.
