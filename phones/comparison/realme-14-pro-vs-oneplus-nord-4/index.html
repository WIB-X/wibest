Realme 14 Pro vs OnePlus Nord 4

Two of the best phones between Rs 25,000 and Rs 35,000 in India — both with flagship-adjacent specs at mid-range prices. Realme 14 Pro targets camera-first buyers with a versatile triple-camera setup. OnePlus Nord 4 is the performance pick with Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3, 100W charging, and the cleanest mid-range software in Android. Here's the full comparison.

Performance: Nord 4 pulls ahead

Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3 in the Nord 4 is a significantly more powerful chip — AnTuTu scores ~1.35 million vs ~800,000 for the Dimensity 7300 Energy. In real use, gaming and multi-tasking on Nord 4 feel closer to a flagship. Realme 14 Pro handles daily tasks smoothly but the performance gap is noticeable in demanding games like BGMI and Call of Duty.

Camera: Realme's versatility vs Nord's consistency

Realme 14 Pro's 50MP ultrawide is a meaningful advantage — a true wide-angle camera at this price is uncommon. Both phones share similar Sony LYT main sensors with OIS. Nord 4's camera tuning is more consistent across scenarios while Realme 14 Pro offers more shooting flexibility. For travel and group shots, Realme wins; for everyday reliability, Nord 4 holds its own.

Battery: Realme's big advantage

Realme 14 Pro's 6000 mAh battery vs Nord 4's 5000 mAh translates to a noticeably longer day. Both have fast charging (80W vs 100W) but the larger capacity means Realme users will charge less frequently. If you're a heavy user who hates running low, Realme 14 Pro has a real edge here.

The verdict

Buy Realme 14 Pro if: you want a versatile triple camera, prefer longer battery life, or are budget-conscious (Rs 4,000 cheaper).

Buy OnePlus Nord 4 if: performance is the priority, you prefer cleaner software (OxygenOS), want the fastest charging, or plan to keep the phone for 3+ years.

Frequently asked questions

Is OnePlus Nord 4 better than Realme 14 Pro?

OnePlus Nord 4 is better for performance and software longevity — Snapdragon 7+ Gen 3 outperforms Dimensity 7300 Energy, and OxygenOS 14 is the cleanest mid-range software available. Realme 14 Pro is better for camera versatility and offers a more affordable entry price.

Which has better software — Realme 14 Pro or OnePlus Nord 4?

OnePlus Nord 4 with OxygenOS is widely considered the most polished, clean Android experience in the mid-range. Realme UI 5.0 is feature-rich but heavier. If you prefer minimal bloatware and smooth animations, Nord 4 is the clear winner.

Does OnePlus Nord 4 have wireless charging?

No, OnePlus Nord 4 does not support wireless charging. It supports 100W wired SUPERVOOC charging which fills the 5000 mAh battery in about 28 minutes — fast enough that wireless charging is rarely missed in daily use.

Which phone has a better display for the price?

Both have excellent 120Hz AMOLED displays. OnePlus Nord 4's 6.74" Super Fluid AMOLED with 2772×1240 resolution and 1600 nits brightness is slightly better calibrated. Realme 14 Pro's display is similarly bright and smooth. Nord 4 edges ahead on colour accuracy.
