Redmi Note 14 Pro vs Poco X7 Pro

Two Xiaomi-family mid-rangers targeting the same buyer under Rs 30,000. Redmi Note 14 Pro is the all-rounder with a best-in-class 200MP camera and premium build. Poco X7 Pro is the performance beast with Dimensity 8400 Ultra, a massive 7000 mAh battery, and gaming-first focus. Here's which to pick.

Camera: Note 14 Pro wins clearly

The 200MP main sensor on the Redmi Note 14 Pro with OIS is the standout camera in this price segment. It captures extraordinary detail in daylight, and the OIS means significantly steadier video. Poco X7 Pro's 50MP camera is decent but simply can't match the detail resolution or low-light OIS advantage. If photography matters, Note 14 Pro is not close.

Performance & gaming: X7 Pro dominates

Dimensity 8400 Ultra in the Poco X7 Pro is a generation ahead — it's more powerful than the chip in last year's flagship OnePlus 12. The Note 14 Pro's Dimensity 7200 Ultra handles everyday tasks fine but throttles heavily in extended gaming. X7 Pro stays cool longer thanks to a larger vapour chamber and its 7000 mAh battery means you can game for 5+ hours on a single charge.

Battery & charging

Poco X7 Pro's 7000 mAh battery is one of the largest in this price segment and the 90W charging refills it in under 50 minutes. Note 14 Pro's 5500 mAh + 45W is competitive but gives you roughly 30% less total runtime on heavy days. The X7 Pro also carries IP68 — full waterproofing versus Note 14 Pro's splash-resistant IP54.

The verdict

Buy Redmi Note 14 Pro if: you prioritise camera quality, prefer a more polished daily experience, and shoot a lot of photos.

Buy Poco X7 Pro if: gaming is your priority, you want the longest battery life, need IP68 waterproofing, or want the most raw performance under Rs 30,000.

Frequently asked questions

Is Poco X7 Pro better than Redmi Note 14 Pro?

Poco X7 Pro wins on gaming and raw performance with its Dimensity 8400 Ultra chip and better cooling. Redmi Note 14 Pro wins on camera quality and MIUI polish for everyday use. Choose X7 Pro for gaming, Note 14 Pro for all-rounder daily use.

Which has a better camera — Redmi Note 14 Pro or Poco X7 Pro?

Redmi Note 14 Pro has the better camera system — 200MP main sensor with OIS delivers noticeably better detail and low-light performance. Poco X7 Pro's 50MP camera is capable but trails in detail and colour accuracy.

Which phone is better for gaming under Rs 30,000?

Poco X7 Pro is the clear gaming winner — Dimensity 8400 Ultra is significantly more powerful than Helio G100, with better GPU performance, lower throttling, and a 7000 mAh battery that supports longer gaming sessions. Note 14 Pro struggles with sustained gaming workloads.

Does Redmi Note 14 Pro have 5G?

Yes, the Redmi Note 14 Pro 5G variant supports 5G with MediaTek Dimensity 7200 Ultra. The base Note 14 Pro uses Helio G100 (4G only). Make sure to check the variant when buying.
