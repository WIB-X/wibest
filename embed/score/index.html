How to use

Where do I paste it?

Anywhere on your website — homepage hero, About page, Contact page footer, or a dedicated "Awards & Recognition" section. The badge is a standard HTML link, so it'll work in any CMS (WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, custom HTML).

Does the badge update if my rating changes?

The rating shown is based on WIB's current verified data at the time the snippet was generated. If your rating changes meaningfully on WIB, just regenerate the snippet from this page and replace it. We don't auto-update the embedded HTML to avoid your site loading external scripts.

Is this an endorsement?

The badge is a statement of fact — your business is listed on WIB with a verified rating. It's not an endorsement, partnership, or paid placement. WIB never accepts payment for listings or ratings.

Can I customise the badge design?

Three styles + three sizes are supported here. For other customisations (colors, languages), the badge HTML is entirely self-contained — edit it freely as long as the link back to your WIB listing remains intact.

My hospital/restaurant isn't on WIB. Can I get listed?

Email hello@wibest.in with your business name, full address, website, and (for hospitals) your NABH accreditation status. We add new businesses to the dataset based on our editorial review.
