How WIB Earns Revenue

WIB (wibest.in) is a free comparison platform. We earn money through affiliate partnerships — when you click a product link on our site and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Our Affiliate Partners

We participate in the following affiliate programs:

  • Amazon Associates Program — WIB is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in. As an Amazon Associate, WIB earns from qualifying purchases.
  • Google AdSense — We display contextual advertisements on some pages through Google AdSense.

How This Affects You

Our affiliate relationships do not affect:

  • The rankings or ratings of any product, school, college, hospital, or service on WIB
  • The price you pay — you pay the same price whether you click through our link or go directly to the retailer
  • Our editorial independence — no brand or company pays us to rank them higher

How to Identify Affiliate Links

Affiliate links on WIB are typically labelled "Buy on Amazon", "Check Price on Amazon", "Check Price on Flipkart", "View on CarDekho", or similar. These links contain tracking parameters that allow the retailer to credit WIB for the referral.

Why We Use Affiliate Links

Affiliate commissions help us keep WIB free for everyone. Without this revenue, we would not be able to maintain the platform, update comparison data, or publish guides and reviews.

Our Commitment

We are committed to providing honest, unbiased comparisons. Our recommendations are based on data, user reviews, and independent research — never on which product pays us the highest commission.
