Hospitals

From WIB's tracked dataset of 463 hospitals across 36 Indian cities.

Which Indian city has the best hospitals?

Bangalore leads WIB's 2026 hospital index — 97% NABH coverage across multi-specialty hospitals and the highest concentration of JCI-accredited tertiary-care centres outside Delhi-NCR.

More: Best Hospitals in Bangalore · State of Indian Hospitals 2026 report

What does NABH accreditation mean?

NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals) is India's flagship hospital quality-accreditation — independently verifying patient safety, infection control, surgical-care, and credentialed-staff standards across 600+ measures.

More: NABH explained in WIB Glossary

Are tier-3 city hospitals worth considering?

Increasingly yes for elective care — 68% of WIB's tracked tier-3 hospitals now hold NABH accreditation versus 97% in metros, and pricing is typically 40-60% lower for the same procedure.

More: Beyond the Metros report

Which Indian city is best for medical tourism?

Chennai for cardiac and ophthalmology; Delhi-NCR for organ transplant and oncology; Mumbai for tertiary trauma — all three lead JCI-accredited capacity outside Bangalore.

Restaurants & Food

From WIB's 605-restaurant dataset across 28 cities.

Which Indian city has the best regional cuisine renaissance?

Indore, Jaipur, and Lucknow lead the tier-3 cuisine revival per WIB's 2026 tracking — driven by next-generation cooks reviving heritage dishes (Indore's chappan, Jaipur's Rajasthani thali, Lucknow's awadhi).

More: Regional Cuisine Renaissance report

Is biryani the same as pulao?

No — biryani is layered (rice and meat cooked separately, then assembled with dum); pulao is one-pot (everything cooks together). Biryani uses more spice volume and saffron-yoghurt marinade.

More: Biryani vs Pulao in Glossary

What is a sadya?

A sadya is the traditional Kerala vegetarian feast served on a banana leaf — 24+ items including rice, sambar, rasam, avial, thoran, payasam, eaten by hand and most associated with Onam festivities.

What is the best Indian city for remote workers in 2026?

Bangalore is the safe pick (mature infrastructure, broadband reliability, English-speaking medical access); Indore is the actually-good pick (lower cost of living, better air quality, similar broadband).

Which Indian metro has the lowest cost of living?

Hyderabad per WIB's 2026 city living index — typically 20-30% lower rent than Bangalore/Mumbai for equivalent neighbourhoods, with broadband and groceries on par.

Bangalore vs Hyderabad — which is better to live in?

Hyderabad wins on cost-of-living and traffic; Bangalore wins on job market depth and weather. The two cities now rank closely on broadband and healthcare; choice mostly comes down to employer concentration in your field.

Delhi on road network, metro coverage, and airport throughput; Mumbai on suburban rail capacity and waste/water reliability. Both cities now have comparable 5G coverage and tier-1 hospital density.

Education

From WIB's 350-school and 365-college datasets across 25 Indian cities.

CBSE vs ICSE — which is better?

Neither is universally better. CBSE aligns with JEE/NEET prep and Indian university entrance; ICSE emphasises English-language depth and is favoured by students targeting humanities, liberal arts, and study-abroad pathways.

More: CBSE vs ICSE in Glossary

Is the IB diploma worth it in India?

Worth it if the student is targeting overseas university admission (US/UK/Canada/Australia) — IB is recognised by 5,000+ universities globally — but adds Rs 8-15 lakh per year over CBSE/ICSE; weak ROI for Indian-college-bound students.

More: IB Diploma in Glossary

What is India's most affordable education city?

Pune and Indore top WIB's 2026 Education Affordability Index — strong school + college density relative to cost of living, with broadband and student-friendly housing infrastructure already in place.

Money & Salary

From WIB's India Salary Index 2026 and Personal Finance glossary.

What is a good CIBIL score for a home loan?

750+ is treated as good by major Indian lenders for home-loan approval; 800+ unlocks the lowest interest rates and largest loan-to-value ratios.

More: CIBIL Score in Glossary

EMI vs SIP — what's the difference?

EMI is an outflow you owe a lender (paying down a loan); SIP is an outflow you invest in your future (buying mutual-fund units monthly). Same monthly debit pattern, opposite balance-sheet effect.

More: EMI · SIP · WIB Calculators

What is the average IT salary in Bangalore in 2026?

Entry-level (0-2 yrs): Rs 6-10 LPA; mid-level (3-7 yrs): Rs 14-28 LPA; senior (8+ yrs): Rs 32-65 LPA — per WIB's 2026 India Salary Index, with product-companies paying 30-50% more than services.

More: India Salary Index 2026

NPS vs PPF — which should I pick?

PPF for capital preservation (sovereign guarantee, fully tax-free at maturity); NPS for higher long-term returns (equity exposure, ₹50k extra 80CCD(1B) deduction over 80C limit). Most salaried Indians use both.

Travel

From WIB's State of Indian Travel 2026 report and travel glossary.

When does Tatkal booking open on IRCTC?

10:00 AM IST for AC classes (1A/2A/3A/CC/EC); 11:00 AM IST for sleeper (SL/2S), exactly one day before the journey date. Booking on popular routes closes within 60-90 seconds.

More: Tatkal in Glossary

What do 1A, 2A, 3A mean on train tickets?

IRCTC AC class codes: 1A First AC (lockable cabins, premium); 2A AC 2-Tier (6 berths/bay, curtained, family choice); 3A AC 3-Tier (8 berths/bay, cheapest AC option).

More: Train AC Classes in Glossary

What is PNR on a train ticket?

PNR (Passenger Name Record) is a 10-digit number identifying your IRCTC booking; shared by all passengers on one booking; used to check confirmed/RAC/WL status. Check free on IRCTC, SMS to 139, or any PNR-status aggregator.
