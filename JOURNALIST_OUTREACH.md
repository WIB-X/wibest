Use these drafts to pitch the two new WIB research reports to Indian journalists. The reports are link-bait gold — concrete data, India-specific, no other source has compiled this for free.

**Your role:** customise the [bracketed] fields and send. Each pitch takes 2 minutes per journalist.

**General sending rules:**
- Send via your personal email (not info@/hello@) — replies happen 5x more
- Subject line drives open rate. Use the exact subjects below.
- Keep emails under 200 words. Journalists scan.
- One pitch per journalist per month. Repeated pitches kill the relationship.
- Pitch one specific data point, not the whole report. Let them ask for more.

> I run wibest.in, an independent India comparison platform. We just published a matched pair of research reports on India's tier-3 cities (Bhopal, Nagpur, Mysore, Vadodara, Patna) — the kind of cross-vertical data nobody compiles for free.
>
> Two findings I thought might interest you:
>
> **Healthcare:** NABH accreditation runs at 68% across 40 tier-3 hospitals we tracked, vs 97% in our metro cohort. That's a 29-percentage-point quality gap. The AIIMS expansion programme is starting to close it in Bhopal and Patna.

Regional-anchored restaurants outscore generic multi-cuisine 4.4 vs 4.3 in average ratings. The strongest single dish in our entire 500-restaurant dataset (4.6/5) is Vinayaka Mylari's butter dosa in Mysore.
>
> Both reports free to cite:
> - Healthcare: https://wibest.in/reports/beyond-the-metros-tier3-hospitals-2026/

> Happy to share underlying datasets (CSV) or break out a specific city if useful.

- LinkedIn/Twitter posts = 50-200 impressions for first attempt; compounds with consistency
- Reddit posts = 500-2,000 views if upvoted, but expect 1 in 4 posts to bomb

## What journalists will ask you for

Be ready with:
1. **Raw data CSV** — export from Firestore or share the data files in your repo
2. **Founder bio + headshot** — already on press kit page
3. **Specific quotes** — already drafted in /press/
4. **Traffic numbers** — GSC impressions for last 28 days
5. **Methodology details** — already in the report's methodology section

## Do NOT

- Don't pay for press releases (PRNewswire / India PR distribution services). Quality journalists ignore PR-distributed content.
- Don't BCC multiple journalists. Always one-to-one.
- Don't follow up more than once. Single follow-up after 5 business days, then drop.
- Don't promise exclusivity unless they explicitly ask.
- Don't ghost replies. Even "thanks but not for us" deserves a 2-line acknowledgment — keeps the relationship.
