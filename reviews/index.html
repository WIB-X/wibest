Reader Reviews & Recommendations

Real opinions from Indians who actually used the place. No paid placements. No fake reviews. No five-star ratings traded for free meals. Just honest signal so you can make better choices.

Why reader reviews matter

Comparison data tells you what something is. Reader reviews tell you what it's actually like. The dosa that "looks good in photos" vs the one your auto driver swears by. The hospital with great equipment vs the one whose nurses actually answer the call button. That's the gap reviews fill.

How WIB handles reviews

✅

Honest only

We never accept paid reviews. Brands cannot pay to remove negative reviews or insert positive ones.

🔍

Verified where possible

We verify reviewer email and order/visit reference where the platform allows it.

📝

Editorial check

Reviews are read by an editor before publishing. Spam, hate speech, and obvious fakes are filtered.

🏷️

Aggregate ratings noted

For restaurants and hospitals, we cross-reference with Google Maps, Zomato, and JustDial signals.

What we look for in a good review

  • Specific details — "great food" helps no one. "The mutton kadai had real bone marrow flavour, took 30 minutes" tells you something.
  • What context you visited in — first-time tourist, regular customer, weekend rush, weekday lunch.
  • The downsides too — every place has weaknesses. Honest reviews call them out.
  • Who would and wouldn't enjoy it — "great for groups, bad for a quiet date" is golden.
  • Money perspective — was the price worth what you got? Compared to alternatives nearby?

Categories we accept reviews for

  • Restaurants & cafes — see /restaurants/ for what we already cover
  • Hospitals — both treatment quality and patient experience matter
  • Schools & colleges — parent and student perspectives both welcome
  • Electronics & appliances — long-term use experience is most useful
  • Travel destinations & stays — what the photos and listings don't tell you
  • Financial & insurance products — claim experience is what matters

The fine print

Submitted reviews may be edited for clarity, length, and grammar — never for sentiment. We will not publish reviews that include defamation, slurs, identifying personal information about staff, or unverifiable serious accusations. By submitting you confirm the review is your own honest experience and grant WIB the right to publish it on this site.
