Two practical rules: never let fuel rewards push you to a farther petrol pump (the detour fuel eats the cashback), and check the monthly cashback cap (most cards cap fuel rewards at Rs 100-500/month — heavy users may need two cards).

Co-branded vs general cashback card for fuel

If fuel is your single biggest card spend, a co-branded card (BPCL SBI, IOC HDFC, HPCL ICICI) wins outright. If fuel is just one of many spends, a strong general cashback card often beats carrying a separate fuel card — see our best credit cards in India guide for the full picture.

The verdict

For sustained fuel spend over Rs 8,000/month, BPCL SBI Octane delivers the highest absolute returns (7.25% on BPCL fuel) but only if BPCL is your usual brand. For brand-flexibility, IDFC FIRST Power+ wins at 6.5% on HPCL with broader benefits. Low-spend users (under Rs 5,000/month fuel) are better served by Axis IOC (free) or ICICI HPCL Coral (Rs 199). Always pick a card matched to your usual fuel brand — savings break down if you switch stations.

Frequently asked questions

Which fuel credit card has the highest cashback?

BPCL SBI Octane offers the highest fuel cashback at 7.25% on BPCL fuel — but only at BPCL stations. For other oil companies, IDFC FIRST Power+ at 6.5% on HPCL is the strongest non-BPCL option.

Do fuel credit cards waive the 1% fuel surcharge?

Yes — all major fuel credit cards waive the standard 1% surcharge that fuel stations charge on credit card transactions. Most have a monthly cap (Rs 100-250) on the waiver amount.

Are fuel credit card annual fees worth it?

For sustained fuel spend of Rs 5,000+/month, yes — the cashback typically exceeds the annual fee within 4-6 months. Most cards waive the annual fee if you spend Rs 50,000-2 lakh/year on the card.

Can I use fuel credit card cashback for non-fuel purchases?

Most fuel cards convert cashback into reward points that can be redeemed for vouchers, statement credit, or merchandise. Some (like Axis Bank IOC) restrict redemption to IOC fuel vouchers only — read terms before applying.

Which is better - co-branded fuel card or general cashback card?

For 80%+ fuel loyalty to one brand, co-branded cards win. For multi-brand users, a general cashback card with 1.5-2% on all spends often wins by simplicity. Calculate based on your annual fuel bill before deciding.
