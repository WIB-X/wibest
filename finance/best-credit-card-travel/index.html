The verdict

For most frequent Indian travellers, HDFC Infinia delivers the best balance of lounge access, reward earning, and acceptance — especially for users with HDFC banking relationship who get the joining fee waived. Axis Magnus is comparable with stronger travel multipliers. Budget travellers should look at Standard Chartered EaseMyTrip (Rs 350 fee) which still gives lounge access. Avoid Amex Platinum unless you spend Rs 5+ lakh/year on travel.

Frequently asked questions

Which credit card gives unlimited lounge access in India?

HDFC Infinia, Axis Bank Magnus, and IndusInd Pinnacle World all offer unlimited domestic + international lounge access via Priority Pass. Most charge Rs 12,500+ joining fee. Mid-tier cards offer 4-12 visits/year.

Is travel credit card worth the annual fee?

For 4+ flights/year and 2+ hotel stays, yes — lounge access alone (worth Rs 1,500-2,500/visit) typically pays back the fee. Add miles + travel insurance + concierge benefits and ROI is strong.

Which travel credit card has lowest forex markup?

IndusInd Pinnacle World at 1.8% is the lowest markup in the premium segment. SBI ELITE at 1.99% is close. Most cards charge 2-3.5% — over a Rs 1 lakh international trip, the difference is Rs 1,500-2,000.

Can I get free domestic flights with credit card miles?

Yes — accumulating 30,000-50,000 miles on cards like HDFC Infinia or Axis Magnus typically unlocks one domestic round-trip economy ticket. Most premium card holders earn this in 4-6 months of typical spend.

What travel insurance comes with credit cards?

Most premium cards include free travel insurance up to Rs 50 lakh-1 crore covering accident, baggage loss, and medical emergencies during international travel. Cover only activates when you book the flight on that card. Read fine print for exclusions.
