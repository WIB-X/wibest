How WIB makes money

WIB is independent and reader-first. Here is exactly how we fund the site — and the lines we never cross. If money ever appears to have influenced what you read here, we’ve failed, and we want to know.

The three rules that never change

  1. Our rankings are never for sale. No company can pay to appear in, rank higher in, or be removed from any WIB comparison. Position is decided by data and our published methodology — nothing else.
  2. Sponsored content is always labeled. If a brand ever pays for visibility, you will see the word “Sponsored” on it, clearly, every time. It sits apart from editorial content and never affects a ranking.
  3. Affiliate links never shape our opinions. Some links earn us a commission if you buy or sign up. Rankings are finalised before any affiliate relationship is considered — an option with no affiliate program ranks exactly where the data puts it.

What we accept

To keep WIB free, independent, and sponsor-free in its rankings, we earn in three transparent ways:

  • Affiliate commissions. When you click through to a retailer, insurer, bank, or platform we compare (Amazon, Flipkart, and similar) and take an action, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. This is disclosed wherever it applies; see our affiliate disclosure.
  • Clearly-labeled sponsor placements. Fixed slots, marked “Sponsored,” shown separately from rankings, with the appropriate link attributes. A sponsor buys visibility, never a position in a list.
  • Advertising that is clearly distinguishable from our content.

What we refuse

✓ Always

  • Rank by data and methodology
  • Label every paid placement
  • Disclose affiliate relationships
  • Correct errors readers report

✗ Never

  • Sell a position in any ranking
  • Run undisclosed “native” ads
  • Take payment to soften an honest assessment
  • Let a sponsor edit editorial content

Why we publish this

Most sites pretend money isn’t involved. We think stating the rules plainly is what actually earns trust. WIB is maintained by a single independent Indian editor — there are no investors and no commercial relationships that influence rankings.

Spot something on WIB that looks like it breaks these rules? Tell us at hello@wibest.in — we treat it as a serious bug, not feedback.
