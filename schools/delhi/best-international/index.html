Frequently asked questions

What is the fee for an international school in Delhi?

International schools in Delhi typically charge Rs 4-15 lakh per year. IB and Cambridge schools at the higher end. One-time admission fees can add Rs 50,000-3 lakh. Total cost from K-12 can be Rs 60 lakh to over Rs 1.5 crore.

Is IB or IGCSE better in Delhi?

IB is more holistic with mandatory CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service) and TOK (Theory of Knowledge). IGCSE is more subject-focused and exam-based. IB is preferred by US universities; IGCSE feeds well into A-Levels for UK universities. Choose based on intended higher-education destination.

Are international school degrees recognised in India?

Yes — IB Diploma and Cambridge A-Levels are recognised by AIU (Association of Indian Universities) for admission to Indian universities. However, scoring conversion can be tricky for some Indian colleges with rigid percentage-based cutoffs.

Which international school in Delhi has the best university placements?

Top international schools in Delhi regularly place students at Ivy League, Oxbridge, top US/UK/Canadian universities, and IISc/Ashoka in India. University placement records are usually published in school annual reports — always request before enrolling.
