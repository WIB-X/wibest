Frequently asked questions

What is the average CBSE school fee in Bhubaneswar?

CBSE school fees in Bhubaneswar range from Rs 50,000 per year at neighbourhood schools to Rs 4-6 lakh per year at premium CBSE schools. Most established CBSE schools fall in the Rs 1-3 lakh annual fee bracket. Capitation fees are illegal but often charged informally — verify carefully.

Is CBSE board better than ICSE in Bhubaneswar?

CBSE is better suited for students preparing for JEE/NEET due to NCERT alignment. ICSE has more emphasis on English and detailed subject coverage. Choice depends on career goals — CBSE for engineering/medical aspirations, ICSE for stronger language and overall foundation.

Which is the best CBSE school in Bhubaneswar?

The top CBSE schools in Bhubaneswar are ranked above based on academic record, faculty experience, infrastructure, and parent reviews. Top schools have strong board exam results, robust extracurricular programs, and 1:25 or better teacher-student ratios.

Do CBSE schools in Bhubaneswar accept mid-year admissions?

Most CBSE schools in Bhubaneswar accept mid-year transfers if seats are available. Admission is usually contingent on TC (transfer certificate) from the previous school and an entrance assessment. Always apply early — popular schools fill seats quickly.
