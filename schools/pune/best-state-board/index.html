Frequently asked questions

Why choose a state board school in Pune?

State board schools are typically more affordable, follow regional language and culture more closely, and offer reservations and quotas for state residents in higher education admissions. Best for students planning state-level professional college admissions.

What is the fee for state board schools in Pune?

State government schools in Pune are largely free or nominal-fee (Rs 2,000-15,000 per year). Private state board schools charge Rs 30,000-2 lakh per year. Mid-tier private state board schools offer good value for families balancing quality with cost.

Are state board exams easier than CBSE?

State board syllabi vary significantly. Some state boards (Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka) have rigorous curricula matching CBSE difficulty. Others may be lighter. Easy/hard comparisons are misleading — what matters is teaching quality at the specific school.

Can state board students appear for JEE/NEET?

Yes — state board students appear for JEE and NEET in large numbers. Many top JEE/NEET rankers come from state board schools. However, additional NCERT-aligned coaching is usually needed since state board syllabi may diverge from JEE/NEET patterns.
