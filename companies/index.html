Frequently asked questions

Which is the best company to work for in India 2026?

No single best — depends on what you optimise for. For raw compensation: Google, Microsoft, Meta, Adobe India (Rs 35-100 LPA at mid-senior level). For learning + brand: McKinsey, BCG, Bain. For Indian-product equity: Razorpay, Cred, Zerodha, Freshworks. For stable career: TCS, Infosys, HDFC, ICICI. For work-life balance: SAP Labs, Microsoft, Atlassian India. Use the search above to filter by what matters to you.

What is the average salary at TCS, Infosys, Wipro in 2026?

Indicative salary bands (TCS / Infosys / Wipro are similar): Entry-level (0-2 yrs) Rs 3.5-5 LPA, Mid (3-7 yrs) Rs 8-15 LPA, Senior (8+ yrs) Rs 20-40 LPA. Product MNCs (Google, Microsoft) pay 2-3x for equivalent experience; Indian startups (Razorpay, Cred) pay 1.5-2x. See the company cards above for specific bands.

Which Indian company has the best work-life balance?

Per aggregated Glassdoor + AmbitionBox reviews 2024-25: SAP Labs India (~4.3/5), Microsoft India (~4.2/5), Atlassian India (~4.2/5), Adobe India (~4.1/5), Zoho (~4.0/5) consistently rank highest on work-life balance among Rs 25+ LPA employers. Consulting (McKinsey/BCG/Bain) and high-growth startups (Razorpay/Cred) compensate more but score 3.2-3.6/5 on WLB.

How accurate are the salary numbers shown on this page?

Salary bands are indicative ranges sourced from publicly-available data (Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, LinkedIn Salary Insights, levels.fyi) as of late 2025. Actual offers vary by role, level, location, and individual negotiation. Use the bands as a starting reference; verify with the company's recruiter or platforms like levels.fyi for tech roles.

Should I optimise for product MNC, Indian startup, or services?

Product MNCs — highest pay, best benefits, slower growth in role. Indian product startups — strong equity upside, faster growth, higher risk, intense work culture. Services — most stable, broader career, lower pay, easier work-life balance. Best choice depends on whether you want stability, learning, or equity wealth.

How often is this dataset updated?

Reviewed semi-annually. Last review: May 2026. Salary bands lag actual market by 3-6 months given how compensation data accumulates publicly. We re-baseline against Glassdoor, AmbitionBox, LinkedIn Salary Insights, and levels.fyi on each review.
